\section{The Phantasm XV: the Arithmetic Metric Is Not Connes's Weighted Norm}
\label{sec:connes-weighted-metric}

Round of 2026-09-22 (brief \texttt{notes/connes-weighted-metric/astra-brief.md}, prover
\texttt{notes/connes-weighted-metric/astra-proofs.md}, numerics \texttt{scripts/connes\_weighted\_metric.py}, review
\texttt{notes/reviews/connes-weighted-metric-2026-09-22.md}). Follows \cref{thm:no-energy-equivalent-metric,prop:unit-coefficient-form}:
the brief conjectured that the unit-coefficient form is Connes's $\delta$-weighted norm restricted to the outgoing model space. It
is not, and the three objects are now separated exactly. Conventions of \cref{sec:deninger-bond}: $K = \KS$ in the Fourier picture,
$e_j = m_{\zero_j} = ik_j$ the named modes ($\langle F,ik_w\rangle = F(w)$), $w_j = \gam{j}/2+ih_j$, $C_\tsg f(X) = f(X+\tsg)$,
$\mathcal C_\Theta F = \Theta\bar F$. RH and simplicity are assumed where stated.

\subsection{The unit-coefficient form}

\begin{theorem}[Biorthogonals, the exact domain, and the representing operator]
\label{thm:unit-coefficient-form-domain}
\claimstatus{thm:unit-coefficient-form-domain}{sketched-conditional}
Under simplicity, $b_j(u) = \Theta(u)/(\Theta'(w_j)(u-w_j)) = \Theta'(w_j)^{-1}\mathcal C_\Theta k_j$ satisfies $\langle e_i,b_j\rangle = \delta_{ij}$,
$\|k_j\|^2 = 1/(2h_j)$, $\|b_j\|^2 = 1/(2h_j|\Theta'(w_j)|^2) = 2/|\Theta'(w_j)|^2$ under RH; the $b_j$ are complete. The synthesis $S_0a = \sum a_je_j$
on $c_{00}$ and the coefficient map $B_0S_0a = a$ on finite sums are closable with mutually inverse closures $S = B^{-1}$;
$\mathcal D(Q_0) = \mathcal D(B) = \{f: \exists a^{(n)}\in c_{00}, a^{(n)}\to a, S_0a^{(n)}\to f\}$, $Bf = (\langle f,b_j\rangle)_j$, $Q_0(f) = \sum|a_j(f)|^2$: an exact
domain with a finite-sum graph core, not an assertion that modal series converge, and not asserted equal to the maximal
domain $\{f:(\langle f,b_j\rangle)\in\ell^2\}$. $Q_0$ is densely defined, closed, positive definite, with $G_0 = B^{*}B$,
$G_0e_j = b_j$ ($B^{*}h = v$ iff $\langle v,e_j\rangle = h_j$ for all $j$), so the modes are not eigenvectors of $G_0$; the all-time
similitude holds on the domain, and the rescaled flow is a unitary group on the arithmetic completion $\ell^2$. The
biorthogonal norm at a zero is the full local Blaschke product, not a nearest-gap rule: a nearest gap gives a sharp lower bound
on one factor and no uniform upper bound.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Connes's norm transported}

\begin{theorem}[Connes's weight on the bond; the weighted Gram; no Riesz bound for any exponent]
\label{thm:connes-norm-transported}
\claimstatus{thm:connes-norm-transported}{sketched-conditional}
Connes's square norm $\int|\xi(g)|^2(1+\log^2|g|)^{\delta/2}d^{*}g$ (\texttt{math/9811068:main.tex:736-753}; ``comparable to''
$(1+|u|)^\delta$, \texttt{:3574-3588}; the representation is ``not unitary'', \texttt{:755-766}) transports, with $X = 2\log|g|$, to
$\|f\|^2_{\delta,2} = \int|f(X)|^2(1+X^2/4)^{\delta/2}dX$ (the brief's $(1+X^2)^{\delta/2}$ is equivalent, not equal), a derivative norm of
order $\delta/2$ in the frequency variable; restricted to $K$ it is a closed form with dense domain containing every full-jet
sum. Under RH, with $\tau_{ij} = (\gam{i}-\gam{j})/2$ and $F_{\delta,c}(s) = \int_0^\infty e^{-sX}(1+X^2/c^2)^{\delta/2}dX$, the weighted Gram of the
modes is $M^{\delta,c}_{ij} = F_{\delta,c}(\tfrac12+i\tau_{ij})$ with constant diagonal $N_{\delta,c} = F_{\delta,c}(\tfrac12)$ and nonzero off-diagonals;
$F_{\delta,c}$ is a Laplace--Struve combination $\mathbf H_{\nu}-Y_{\nu}$, $\nu = \delta/2+\tfrac12$, or the absolutely convergent Tricomi series
$c\sum_n(-2)^n\binom{\delta/2}n n!\,U(n+1,\delta-n+2,cs)$, and for even $\delta = 2m$ the finite sum $\sum_r\binom mr(2r)!/(c^{2r}s^{2r+1})$; the tail
is $F_{\delta,c}(\alpha+i\tau) = 1/(\alpha+i\tau)+\delta/(c^2(\alpha+i\tau)^3)+O(|\tau|^{-5})$, so $|M^{\delta,c}_{ij}|\sim2/|\gam{i}-\gam{j}|$: the cut at zero
leaves a jump and the brief's $|\gam{i}-\gam{j}|^{-1-\delta}$ decay is refuted. For every fixed real $\delta$ and $c>0$: no lower Riesz bound
($\|v_i-v_j\|^2_{\delta,c}\le\tau_{ij}^2\int X^2e^{-X/2}w\,dX/N\to0$ along vanishing gaps) and no upper bound (clusters of $n$ ordinates in an
interval where $\re R\ge\tfrac12$ give $\|\sum v_j\|^2\ge n^2/2$ against $n$): no Schur threshold in $\delta$; the weighted form and $Q_0$
satisfy neither inequality on finite modal sums, hence are not equivalent, and a diagonal-conformal metric would need
distinct frequencies orthogonal, which the weighted Gram violates.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Connes's cokernel dual and the cut-and-damp map]
\label{thm:connes-cokernel-cut-damp}
\claimstatus{thm:connes-cokernel-cut-damp}{sketched}
Connes's $E(f)(g) = |g|^{1/2}\sum_{q\in\mathbb Q^{*}}f(qg)$ is an isometry with closed range on the completed Schwartz functions with
$f(0) = \int f = 0$ (\texttt{:773-808,3760-3791}); the anti-dual of its $\delta>1$ cokernel is the closed annihilator
$\mathcal N_\delta\subset H_{-\delta} = L^2(w_{\delta,2}^{-1}dX)$ (bilinear pairing at \texttt{:3656-3678,3795-3835}), and
$\mathcal N_\delta = \overline{\mathrm{span}}\{\chi_{\gamma,r}(X) = X^re^{-i\gamma X/2}/r!: 0\le r<m_\gamma,\ r<(\delta-1)/2\}$: the uncut critical
characters and jets (annihilator equation $L(\tfrac12+it)\hat\psi = 0$, \texttt{:4012-4017}; spectral localisation \texttt{:4058-4087};
sufficiency \texttt{:4106-4136}; density via approximate units \texttt{:4145-4149}); the integrability condition is $2r-\delta<-1$ (his
line \texttt{:4100} prints $2k+\delta<-1$, a typo), so the multiplicity is the largest $n\le m_\gamma$ with $n<(1+\delta)/2$, $r = 1$ needing
$\delta>3$ strictly; the dual characters are $|g|^{\pm i\gamma}$, the $-\tfrac12$ belonging to the additive normalisation. The map
$(Th)(X) = 1_{X>0}e^{-X/4}h(X)$ is bounded $H_{-\delta}\to L^2(w_{\eta,2}dX)$ for every $\eta$, sends $\chi_{\gamma,r}$ to the model jet
$e_{\zero,r}$, so $T\mathcal N_\delta\subset K\cap\bigcap_\eta L^2(w_{\eta,2}dX)$ with dense range under RH and simplicity (a proper closed
span otherwise), is injective on $\mathcal N_\delta$, and $C_\tsg T = e^{-\tsg/4}TR_\tsg$ ($R_\tsg$ translation). It is not the inverse of kernel
evaluation: $A_e = -i\mathcal A = S_0^{*}$, $A_e^{*} = S$, $B^{*} = A_e^{-1}$, $\mathcal A^{-1}\delta_j = -ib_j$, whereas $T(\sum a_j\chi_{\gam{j},0}) = S_0a$:
synthesis on the finite coordinates, with model conjugation the extra antilinear operation. Domain separation: in $H_{-\delta}$,
$\|\chi_{\gam{i},0}-\chi_{\gam{j},0}\|\to0$ along vanishing gaps while the arithmetic coefficient norm stays $\sqrt2$, so $T$ does not extend
boundedly into $\ell^2$ and $T\mathcal N_\delta\not\subset\mathcal D(Q_0)$ (closed graph): membership in every polynomially weighted outgoing
norm does not give membership in the arithmetic domain. All cut-and-damped jets are in $K$ unconditionally; the uncut dual
jet of order $r$ needs $\delta>2r+1$.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Exits and the classification}

\begin{proposition}[One scalar exit versus an exit coordinate per mode; the metric classes]
\label{prop:two-exits-two-metrics}
\claimstatus{prop:two-exits-two-metrics}{sketched-conditional}
On the generator domain $-\langle f',g\rangle-\langle f,g'\rangle = f(0)\overline{g(0)}$ (\cref{lem:exit-one-dimensional}), $j(e_j) = 1$, the energy Gram
$M_{ij} = 2/(1+i(\gam{i}-\gam{j}))$ under RH with loss $-(d_i+\bar d_j)M_{ij} = 1$, and the exit is an isometric observation history
$(\mathcal Of)(s) = f(s)$, $\|f\|^2 = \int_0^\infty|j(C_sf)|^2ds$; on modal coordinates $M^{T} = \int_0^\infty e^{sD^{*}}\mathbf 1\mathbf 1^{*}e^{sD}ds$,
$D^{*}H+HD = -\mathbf 1\mathbf 1^{*}$: the Cauchy metric is the all-ones exit integrated over the whole history. On the arithmetic completion
$D_{\mathrm{ar}} = -\tfrac14+i\,\mathrm{diag}(-\gam{j}/2)$, $-(D_{\mathrm{ar}}+D_{\mathrm{ar}}^{*}) = \tfrac12I$, $I-C_{\tsg,\mathrm{ar}}^{*}C_{\tsg,\mathrm{ar}} = (1-e^{-\tsg/2})I$: the
minimal instantaneous exit space is $\ell^2$. The metric cannot be obtained by conjugating with the exit: the summation row
$\ell a = \sum a_j$ is non-invertible and non-closable on $\ell^2$; the replacement is $G_0 = (S^{-1})^{*}S^{-1} = B^{*}B$, $Q_0(Sa) = \|a\|^2$
(on a finite family $H_F^{-1}$ in modal coordinates; $G_0 = UH_S^{-1}U^{*}$ by polar decomposition). The finite-time energy defect
$(M_\tsg)_{ij} = (1-e^{\tsg(d_i+\bar d_j)})/(-(d_i+\bar d_j))$ is positive definite on every finite family (infinite rank): rank one refers
to the instantaneous form only. Classification: the metrics with a scalar generator exit on fixed modes are exactly the
diagonal congruences $G^c_{ij} = c_i\bar c_j/(-(d_i+\bar d_j))$, $c_i\ne0$ (many metrics; the energy Gram is the one with $j(e_i) = 1$),
while the all-time similitude cone under RH and simplicity is $G(e_i,e_j) = g_i\delta_{ij}$ with loss $\tfrac12g_i\delta_{ij}$ of rank
equal to the number of modes: no similitude metric has a rank-one loss when two modes are present, and no scalar-exit
metric is in the cone. The polynomially weighted metric has loss $f(0)\overline{g(0)}+\int_0^\infty w'f\bar g\,dX$, boundary plus bulk,
a third object.
\end{proposition}

\begin{observation}[Three metrics; the required Hodge metric; what is open]
\label{obs:three-metrics-dictionary}
\claimstatus{obs:three-metrics-dictionary}{sketched}
Energy (cut damped characters and all jets; Cauchy Gram; one scalar boundary trace; $C_\tsg$ a contraction; modes complete and
minimal, not Riesz), arithmetic unit-coefficient (the same modes made orthonormal under RH and simplicity; identity Gram;
loss $I/2$; $e^{-\tsg/4}$ times a unitary group on $\ell^2$; unbounded comparison both ways), Connes's weighted realisation (uncut
characters and admitted jets in the weighted dual; on cut modes the Gram $F_{\delta,2}$; boundary plus bulk loss; polynomially
bounded non-unitary translations; $\delta$ controls admissibility of uncut jets at infinity, not which cut jets have finite norm,
which all do). If ``positive Hodge metric'' means a positive metric with the conformal evolution of
\cref{thm:bond-positive-metric-criterion}, its form is diagonal in the simple critical modes, and with the normalised pairing and
star it is $Q_0$; without a fixed cup normalisation the positive diagonal family remains. Open: a construction from theta,
adelic or geometric data, without listing the zeros, of a closed positive form on the outgoing model whose graph domain is
$\mathcal D(B)$, whose pairing restricts to the unit form, and which obeys the similitude; Connes's polynomial topology does not
supply it.
\end{observation}
