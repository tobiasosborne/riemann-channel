\subsection*{2026-09-13: finite BC symmetry constraints}

TJO clarified that the BC state is the prescribed stationary state of an
unknown Lindbladian, constrained by Galois symmetry and adelic symplectic
structure. The finite inverse problem was formulated as a linear section
of the conditional Choi cone in \cref{sec:bc-symmetry-generators}. At
criticality its dimensions were computed for Galois, linear Weil, and
additional Weyl covariance; the last must be distinguished from merely
carrying the symplectic representation. The finite BC phase marginal does
not specify a matrix-state extension, and an exact finite representation
of the full BC algebra cannot retain nontrivial phase observables.

An explicit $p=7$ covariant GKLS generator has unequal odd decay rates.
Compatible two-prime models retain a free coupling even with fixed local
semigroups. The numerical script passes $427$ checks and is included in
local CI. Full written derivations were supplied by Codex but no independent
review was performed, so the eight new analytic claim rows are
\texttt{sketched}; the ninth is \texttt{numerical}. The arithmetic map to
the scattering bond and the adelic limit remain open.

\subsection*{2026-09-13: the fermion parity of a cMPS ring}
