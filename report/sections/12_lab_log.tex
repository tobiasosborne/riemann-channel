\section{Lab Log}

The dated worklogs under \texttt{docs/worklog/} are the primary chronology;
\texttt{HANDOFF.md} is the live state. This section is their mirror at the
level of decisions.

\subsection*{2026-09-10/11: founding session}

One conversation in the parent repository
\texttt{arithmetic-quantum-mechanics}. Trajectory: Bost--Connes basics; why
not Wick-rotate to Hilbert--P\'olya; an elliptic curve over $\mathbb F_2$ as a
finite instance; permutations and the one determinant identity; automata and
rationality; the actual Riemann hypothesis as a flow with orbit periods
$\log p$; the Ihara zeta of regular graphs as the vestigial Selberg zeta;
Shor's unitary as the level-$N$ Galois symmetry of Bost--Connes; quantum
expanders and the quantum Ihara zeta; matrix-product states and the ring-norm
reading; the Bost--Connes system as a matrix-product operator over the prime
chain; the Holevo--Werner lift; and the computation of the scattering symbol,
its zeros, and the $3000$-zero ring-norm test.

Corrections TJO made along the way, kept as working rules: stay elementary,
no jargon dumps, one step at a time; no curves or field extensions when a
permutation will do; ``QFT'' did not mean functional integrals; length
quantisation should come from a flatness-type condition.

Afternoon: the Weil--LPS channels computed for seven primes. Bugs found and
fixed: quaternion enumeration starting at an odd coordinate (caught by the
divisor-sum count); parity as a second fixed point on the full space
(resolved by splitting into the two Weil blocks); the Hecke relation failing
on $\mathrm{SL}_2$ by signs (resolved on $\mathrm{PSL}_2$).

Evening: the matrix-product formulation of the Weil conjectures for quadratic
Artin--Schreier curves. Bugs: a wrong irreducibility test for composite $n$;
the even-$n$ sign, which was the $L$-function normalisation, not a bug.

\subsection*{2026-09-12}

Morning: circle flows discussed (no code); the expository note on Deligne's
proof written; repository made public under AGPL-3.0.

Afternoon: prior-art search on the quantum Ihara zeta, three Opus literature
agents, then byte-verification against arXiv TeX sources (\texttt{refs/}).
Finding: the object and its determinant formula are known
(Matsuura--Ohta; Watanabe--Fukumizu for loopless graphs; Sunada); the
RH-for-channels reading, the MPS dictionary and the Weil-representation
instance were not found. Then the Ihara--Bass formula for arbitrary Kraus
families proved in Lamport style and taken through two rounds of adversarial
review by Opus reviewers; promoted to \texttt{proved}. Standing preference
recorded: TeX source, not PDF, for every quotation.

Evening: this lab book set up following the parent repository's sharded
pattern, with the notation, definitions, claims and provenance databases
under \texttt{db/}, the generated macro and status files, the gate
\texttt{scripts/labbook\_check.py}, the local CI \texttt{scripts/ci\_local.sh}
and its pre-commit hook. Notation and definitions across the seven notes
de-duplicated into the databases; the clashes and their resolutions are
listed in \cref{sec:conventions}.

Night: the Selberg dictionary campaign (codex prover, Opus refute review, shards
09b/09c), and the Weil-positivity campaign of the second afternoon session (shards
08b/08c). Evening: TJO set the Bost--Connes reframe as the central priority and named the
chased object the Phantasm.

\subsection*{2026-09-12, night: the Phantasm campaign}

Orchestration: one codex prover session (\texttt{gpt-6-astra}, xhigh; brief
\texttt{notes/riemann-cmps/astra-brief.md}, proofs
\texttt{notes/riemann-cmps/astra-proofs.md}, 1356 lines, labels T1--T8), three Opus
lanes in parallel (Artin--Schreier numerics, Riemann-side numerics, TeX sources with
57 byte-verified quotes and 17 new arXiv sources), then an Opus REFUTE review. Results:
\cref{sec:phantasm-forced}, \cref{sec:phantasm-channels},
\cref{sec:artin-schreier-super}, the definitions shard \cref{sec:definitions-c}, and the
restatements of \cref{prop:ringnorm-trace} (HANDOFF item 1c) and
\cref{prop:as-transfer-matrix}. Corrections along the way: the founding session's
$\alphaw{i} = -\lambda_i(\Tmat)$ was found false by the numerics lane; the orchestrator's
replacement (a conjugated law) was found false by the same lane in two further cases; the
prover produced the exact law (radical sign, Stickelberger on the complement) and the
numerics lane confirmed it in $624$ further checks. TJO's steering during the campaign: the
fixed point of the bond Lindbladian is literally the entanglement spectrum, so the Gibbs
entropy and its cutoffs are entanglement data (recorded; the Hagedorn level density rules
out a CFT reading); and the Phantasm must carry the Bost--Connes symmetry
$\hat{\mathbb Z}^\times$, which led to the trivial-versus-nontrivial-character reading of
the grading and to \cref{conj:galois-graded-bond}.
