\subsection{The dictionary}

\begin{proposition}[The Tate-twist identity of graded spectral data]
\label{prop:deninger-twist-identity}
\claimstatus{prop:deninger-twist-identity}{proved}
Let $D = \{(0,+),(1,+)\}\cup\{(\zero,-m_\zero)\}$ be Deninger's graded spectral datum for $\mathbb Q$ in his time,
with archimedean term $W_\infty(t_D) = (1-e^{-2t_D})^{-1}$, and $N = \{(0,+)\}\cup\{(-\bar\zero/2,-m_\zero)\}\cup
\{(-(k+\tfrac12),-)\}_{k\ge1}$ the notebook's datum (\cref{def:riemann-graded-bond}). (a) The twist $H(1)$ followed
by $\tsg = 2t_D$, $\lambda\mapsto(\lambda-1)/2$, sends $D$ to $\{(-\tfrac12,+),(0,+)\}\cup\{(-\bar\zero/2,-m_\zero)\}$;
this is the transport printed under \cref{cor:forced-net-spectrum}. (b) $e^{-t_D}$ times Deninger's Lefschetz
formula $1-\sum_\zero e^{t_D\zero}+e^{t_D} = \sum_\pp\log\pp\sum_{k\ge1}\delta_{k\log\pp}+W_\infty$ is
$1+e^{-\tsg/2}-\Trd\Zt = 2\primeP(\tsg)+\sum_{k\ge0}e^{-(k+1/2)\tsg}$, which is \cref{prop:ringnorm-trace}
rearranged: the two identities are the same statement. (c) As net multiplicities
(\cref{thm:supertrace-rigidity}) the twisted $D$ plus the archimedean term counted odd equals $N$; the
cancellation at $-\tfrac12$ is what Deninger's distinct-eigenvalue hypothesis excludes, so $N$ is a net datum,
not a Deninger datum. (d) $D$ is the divisor of $\zeta\,\Gamma_{\mathbb R}$, $\Gamma_{\mathbb R}(s) =
\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)$; the comb datum of \cref{cor:forced-net-spectrum} is the divisor of $\zeta$; the difference is
the divisor of $\Gamma_{\mathbb R}$, even poles at $-2k$, $k\ge0$, which is the trace of $e^{t_D\Theta}$ on Deninger's
$R_\infty = \mathbb R[e^{-2y}]$, $\Theta = d/dy$; the notebook's odd ladder is the trivial zeros of $\zeta$, odd
because they are zeros, and the parity freedom of \cref{prop:ladder-parity-free} is the choice of which side of
the trace formula carries the archimedean place. A complex place has one simple pole of $\Gamma_{\mathbb C}$ per
nonpositive integer, and $\Gamma_{\mathbb C}(s) = \Gamma_{\mathbb R}(s)\Gamma_{\mathbb R}(s+1)$ is two real ladders.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Suspension = ring; the zeros as joint eigenvalues; the Bost--Connes shadow]
\label{prop:deninger-suspension-ring}
\claimstatus{prop:deninger-suspension-ring}{proved}
(a) For $X = M\times_{\qs^{\mathbb Z}}\mathbb R^{>0}$ with $M$ a compact manifold and $\qs$ acting by a diffeomorphism
$f^{-1}$, the reduced leafwise cohomology is the space of smooth sections $u\mapsto c(u)\in H^i(M)$ with
$c(\qs u) = f^*c(u)$, $\Theta = u\,d/du$ has spectrum $\{(\log\mu+2\pi ik)/\log\qs\}$ over $\mu\in\operatorname{spec}(f^*|H^i(M))$, % bare-ok
and $\sum_\lambda e^{t\lambda} = \log\qs\sum_{m\in\mathbb Z}\Tr(f^{*m}|H^i(M))\,\delta(t-m\log\qs)$ by Poisson summation:
the periodic MPS ring of length $\log\qs$ with site tensor $f^*$. A lifted Frobenius is a covering of degree
$|\pi|^2 = \pp$, not a diffeomorphism; Deninger's solenoid $\mathbb C\times_\Gamma T_\pi\Gamma$ with the shift gives
the same Lefschetz data, and the net odd spectrum $\{s : \pp^s\in\{\pi,\bar\pi\}\}+2\pi i\mathbb Z/\log\pp$, the zeros
of $\curvezeta$ with their towers. (b) (Formal.) For $\mathbb Q^{>0}$ acting by commuting Frobenii $F_\pp^*$, the
$\Theta$-eigenvectors are $u^\zero v$ with $F_\pp^*v = \pp^\zero v$ for all $\pp$; density of $\mathbb Q^{>0}$ collapses
the towers to points; a product leaf space admits a common exponent only where every local spectrum contains
$\pp^\zero$, which for Euler factors forces $\zero = 0$ (\cref{obs:prime-by-prime-blind}); the 2018 construction
couples the primes through the adele topology, not through a joint eigenbasis. (c) On $\ltwo{\mathbb N}$,
$v_\zero = \sum_nn^{-\zero}e_n$ satisfies $\bciso{\pp}^*v_\zero = \pp^{-\zero}v_\zero$ for all $\pp$ and every
$\re\zero>\tfrac12$, with $\|v_\zero\|^2 = \zeta(2\re\zero)$; nothing in $\ltwo{\mathbb N}$ selects the zeros, and a
metric in which $\pp^{-1/2}F_\pp^*$ is unitary for all $\pp$ makes $v_\zero\perp v_{\zero'}$ unless
$\zero+\bar\zero' = 1$, two primes with $\log\pp/\log\qs$ irrational sufficing.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[The local Lefschetz coefficient; a rotation angle per prime]
\label{prop:deninger-local-coefficient}
\claimstatus{prop:deninger-local-coefficient}{proved}
Let $A\in\mathrm{GL}(2,\mathbb R)$ be the linearised Poincar\'e return map on the leaf plane at a closed orbit of
length $l$. (a) Deninger's coefficient of $\delta_{kl}$ is $\det(1-A^k)/|\det(1-A^k)|$ for $k\ge1$ and
$\det(A^k)\det(1-A^{-k})/|\det(1-A^{-k})|$ for $k\le-1$, with $\det(1-B) = \sum_i(-1)^i\Tr\Lambda^iB$ the graded trace % bare-ok
of the exterior algebra of the leaf plane; the coefficient keeps only its sign. (b) For $A = e^{l/2}O$, $O\in\mathrm O(2)$:
the coefficient is $1$ for all $k\ge1$ iff $O\in\mathrm{SO}(2)$; for $\det O = -1$ it is $(-1)^k$; for $k\le-1$ it is
$e^{kl}$ in both cases. (c) For $O$ the rotation by $\omega$ and $e^l = \pp$, the spectrum of $\Lambda^*A$ is % bare-ok
$\{1,\sqrt\pp e^{\pm i\omega},\pp\}$, the Frobenius spectrum of an elliptic curve over $\Fp$ with trace
$2\sqrt\pp\cos\omega$ whenever that is an integer (Deuring, not byte-cited); the angle is invisible to the Ruelle
zeta and is data the explicit formula never sees.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[The metric is a flow-invariant polarisation of the symplectic odd bond]
\label{thm:deninger-invariant-polarisation}
\claimstatus{thm:deninger-invariant-polarisation}{proved}
Let $V$ be a real vector space, $B$ a nondegenerate alternating form (the cup product $H^1\times H^1\to H^2\cong\mathbb R$),
and $\Theta$ with $B(\Theta h,h')+B(h,\Theta h') = c\,B(h,h')$. (i) $e^{-ct/2}e^{t\Theta}\in\Sp(V,B)$, the
$B$-transpose is $\Theta^\# = c-\Theta$, and the spectrum is symmetric under $\lambda\mapsto c-\lambda$: the functional
equation without RH; in finite dimension a non-canonical similarity realises the operator form (FE) of
\cref{def:graded-rh-fe-ramanujan}. (ii) A polarisation is $*$ with $*^2 = -1$, $B(*h,*h') = B(h,h')$ and
$(h,h') := B(h,*h')$ positive definite; if $[\Theta,*] = 0$ then $\Theta = c/2+S$ with $S$ skew for $(\,,)$, the
manifest form (HP) with $G = (\,,)$ (\cref{prop:hp-inner-product-continuous}), after which nothing remains, unlike
\cref{obs:kraus-dichotomy}(d), where Hastings' bound remains; conversely (HP) with a $B$-compatible form is a
flow-invariant polarisation. (iii) In leaf dimension two the star on 1-forms depends only on the conformal class:
the hypothesis is a flow-invariant leafwise complex structure with area scaled by $e^{ct}$. (iv) For
$e^{-c/2}e^\Theta$ semisimple with eigenvalues $e^{\pm i\omega_j}$, $\omega_j\in(0,\pi)$ pairwise distinct, the invariant
polarisation is unique; for a repeated $e^{i\omega}$ with Krein signature $(p,q)$ the invariant polarisations form
$\mathrm U(p,q)/(\mathrm U(p)\times\mathrm U(q))$, a point iff $pq = 0$; at $\omega\in\{0,\pi\}$ the Siegel space of the
eigenspace; none if an eigenvalue is off the circle. (v) Invariant alternating forms and invariant metrics have the
same dimension, so $B$ removes the cone's freedom (\cref{thm:arithmetic-metric}) only when $B$ is canonical;
\cref{prop:d2-spectrum-frobenius} gives $K_{\mathrm{HW}}\simeq H^1\otimes\mathbb C^2$ as a spectral similarity only, so no cup
form on $K_{\mathrm{HW}}$ is defined by the notebook.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}[Compact manifolds force $c = 0$; what the solenoid changes]
\label{prop:deninger-alpha-zero-solenoid}
\claimstatus{prop:deninger-alpha-zero-solenoid}{proved}
(a) On a compact oriented manifold with a transversal flow and its invariant closed one-form $\omega_\phi$
($\omega_\phi|_{T\mathcal F} = 0$, $\omega_\phi(Y_\phi) = 1$), conformality on $T\mathcal F$ with factor $e^{ct}$ forces $c = 0$:
$\Omega = \mathrm{vol}_{\mathcal F}\wedge\omega_\phi$ is a volume form with $\phi^{t*}\Omega = e^{ct}\Omega$ and invariant integral. % bare-ok
(b) On the elliptic solenoid with $g = e^t\re(\xi\bar\eta)$ the gluing $(z,y,t)\sim(\pi^{-1}z,\pi^{-1}y,t+\log\pp)$ is a % bare-ok
$g$-isometry preserving flat area times Haar measure; the flow scales $g$-area by $e^s$ and the transverse measure
$e^{-t}\,d\mathrm{Haar}\,dt$ by $e^{-s}$; the return map $(z,y)\mapsto(\pi z,\pi y)$ expands $z$ and contracts $y$, and
the $\Omega$ of (a) does not exist. In the notebook's normalisations this is the counting growth $\qs$ against the % bare-ok
channel growth $1$ (\cref{def:graded-transfer-channel}); the adelic objects of \cref{sec:gl1-bond} are not
suspensions of this type ($\mathbb A/\mathbb Q$ has no closed orbits; $\mathbb Q^{>0}\hat{\mathbb Z}^\times\times_{\mathbb Q^{>0}}\mathbb R^{>0}$
is irreducible, \cref{cit:deninger-2018-connected-h0}).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Deninger's elliptic example is the Hodge ket bond]
\label{prop:deninger-elliptic-hodge-ket}
\claimstatus{prop:deninger-elliptic-hodge-ket}{proved-conditional}
For an ordinary $E/\Fp$ with Frobenius $\pi$ and CM lift (\cref{asm:deuring-lift}; Deninger takes the maximal order):
(a) Deninger's leaf-space data $(H^*(\mathbb C/\Gamma),\pi^*,(-1)^{\deg})$ is the doubled bond
$(V\otimes\bar V,\Edb = \operatorname{diag}(1,\bar\pi,\pi,\pp),\Gdb)$ of \cref{thm:hodge-doubling-tensor}, his $H^1$ the odd sector
and $H^0\oplus H^2$ the even; (b) his Hodge inner product on $H^1$ is the doubled ket-bond inner product on the odd
sector; (c) conformality with factor $e^t$ per period $\log\pp$ is $\|\pi^*dz\|^2 = \pp\|dz\|^2$, the ket-bond letter
$1\oplus\sqrt\pp\,U$ with $U$ unitary on $V_-$, which is \cref{thm:elliptic-ph-unitary}; (d) the net odd
$\Theta$-spectrum of his solenoid is the zero set of $\curvezeta$ with towers, its trace formula the explicit formula of
$\curvezeta$. At genus one the metric test is vacuous (\cref{obs:gl1-bond-no-metric}) and Deninger says the same
(\cref{cit:deninger-lift-misleading}); at higher genus \cref{thm:rosati-ph-genus-two} has the compatibility free and
the positivity as input, Deninger's Theorem 4 the positivity free and the compatibility as input.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[$\KS$ is not Deninger's $H^1$ with an equivalent inner product]
\label{thm:ks-not-deninger-h1}
\claimstatus{thm:ks-not-deninger-h1}{proved}
Let $w_n = -\gam n/2-i(1-\sigs_n)/2$ be the zeros of $\Ssym$ in the lower half plane and $k_n(\tauM) = (\tauM-\bar w_n)^{-1}$
the reproducing kernels, which lie in $\KS$. (a) In Deninger's picture, with $(\,,)$ positive and $\Theta-\tfrac12$
skew-adjoint on the completion, eigenvectors at distinct zeros are orthogonal, and under pure point spectrum (an
assumption) the completion is $\ltwo{\text{zeros}}$ with $\Theta$ diagonal. (b) The normalised $k_n$ are not a Riesz
basis of their closed span: the Carleson condition fails, since the pseudo-hyperbolic distance of consecutive modes
satisfies $\varrho(w_n,w_{n+1})^2 = (d_n/2)^2/((d_n/2)^2+\tfrac14)\le d_n^2$ under RH for a gap $d_n$ and % bare-ok
$\liminf d_n = 0$, and unconditionally the on-line zeros (a positive proportion, Selberg, not byte-cited) supply such
pairs; the Riesz-basis criterion for kernels of a model space (Shapiro--Shields, Nikolski, not byte-cited) is the
Carleson condition. Hence no bounded invertible map sends an orthonormal eigenbasis of Deninger's $\Theta$ to
multiples of the $k_n$, and no inner product on $\KS$ equivalent to the Hardy one makes $\Zt$ normal with the $k_n$
as eigenvectors. For two normalised kernels $1-|\langle\hat k_a,\hat k_b\rangle|^2 = \varrho(a,b)^2$, so % bare-ok
$\sigma_{\min}$ of the normalised Gram matrix is at most $1-\sqrt{1-\varrho_{\min}^2}$; on the first $3000$ zeros % bare-ok
$\varrho_{\min} = 0.097$ and $\sigma_{\min} = 0.40, 0.23, 0.13, 0.075, 0.040, 0.016$ for $N = 10, 25, 50, 100, 200, 400$ % bare-ok
(\texttt{notes/deninger-cmps/scratch\_riesz.py}, reproduced by the reviewer to three digits). The Gram data
$\langle k_n,k_m\rangle = i/(\bar w_n-w_m)$ of \cref{prop:mode-diagonal-rebound-rh} is what $\KS$ carries and
Deninger's $H^1$ discards; H-THETA (\cref{conj:h-theta}), if true, identifies $\KS$ with something that is not
Deninger's $H^1$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}[The bond as an algebra: where the derivation mechanism can act]
\label{prop:deninger-product-genus-one}
\claimstatus{prop:deninger-product-genus-one}{proved-conditional}
(a) Deninger's $\phi^{t*}$ is a ring automorphism of leafwise cohomology, so $\Theta$ is a derivation and the Leibniz
rule on $H^1\cup H^1\to H^2$ with $\Theta = c$ on $H^2$ is the functional-equation half of
\cref{thm:deninger-invariant-polarisation}(i). (b) A completely positive map on $\operatorname{End}(V)$ is
multiplicative for composition iff it is $\Ad(U)$ with $U$ unitary (Skolem--Noether and Choi, not byte-cited), and
multiplicative up to a scalar iff it is $c'\Ad(U)$; composition is not Deninger's product. (c) At genus one the
doubled bond is $H^*(\mathbb T^2,\mathbb C) = \Lambda^*(\mathbb C\,dz\oplus\mathbb C\,d\bar z)$ with the exterior product, and for it % bare-ok
$A\otimes\bar A$ is a ring endomorphism for every even letter $A = 1\oplus a$, an automorphism iff $a\ne0$, although $\operatorname{diag}(1,\pi)$ is not
a scalar times a unitary: Deninger's derivation argument applies verbatim, with $B$ the $(-,-)$ coefficient of the
wedge of two odd vectors, and concludes \cref{thm:elliptic-ph-unitary}. At genus two the doubled bond
$\mathbb C^{1|2}\otimes\overline{\mathbb C^{1|2}}$ is not the cohomology ring of the curve (\cref{sec:ring-norm-genus-two}); whether any other bond
of this book carries a graded-commutative product for which its transfer is multiplicative up to scale is open.
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Verdict and next steps}

\begin{observation}[Same gap, stated more sharply]
\label{obs:deninger-same-gap}
\claimstatus{obs:deninger-same-gap}{sketched}
No candidate metric emerges beyond the inequivalent diagonal form on mode coefficients, which is RH. Deninger's
mechanism is (HP) with the functional equation from the cup product; his own words place the conformal metric out
of reach for number fields (\cref{cit:deninger-conformal-metric}), the realised example at the notebook's
genus-one vacuity (\cref{cit:deninger-lift-misleading}), and the 2018 spaces without analytic structure
(\cref{cit:deninger-2018-no-analytic}), over Connes's adele space (\cref{cit:deninger-2018-adeles}), with their one
cohomological fact from an irreducible leaf space (\cref{cit:deninger-2018-connected-h0}); his second mechanism,
a Laplacian with $-(\Theta-\tfrac12)^2$ as its restriction (\texttt{math/0505354:main.tex:852-869}), has the same
equivariance gap and a metric cone of dimension $3g$. What the translation adds: the metric as a flow-invariant
polarisation, a point given simple spectrum and a canonical $B$; the notebook has a canonical $B$ only on the
genus-one Hodge ket; the archimedean ladder as $R_\infty$; the prime letters as one flow sampled at $\log\pp$, the
blind ansatz unless the leaf space is non-product, with one rotation angle per prime as data $\zeta$ does not fix;
and $\KS$ not Deninger's $H^1$ under any equivalent norm. In the comparative table of the note, the attacks with a
metric (Artin--Schreier, Weil, Deninger on elliptic curves) have it given in advance or from a product on the bond
with a positivity supplied from outside, and the attacks without a product (channels, Lax--Phillips, Connes) have
one-sided bounds or criteria.
\end{observation}
