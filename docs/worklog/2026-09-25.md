## Afternoon: adeles, discussion round

TJO: "i want to talk about the adeles ... very mysterious to me"; (1) what software exists, in general; (2) if `A_K`
were a first-class citizen like `Q` or `R`, are the algorithms known in the literature and just not implemented?
Recorded in `notes/adeles/software-and-algorithmic-scope.md` (discussion, from memory, not byte-verified; nothing
registered). Answers: only Hertogh's Sage `adeles` package has the ring as a type; everything else uses one completion,
a finite quotient of the idele class group, or a finite double-coset set. The algorithms are known: `A_K` is glued by
CRT from `K`, real balls and profinite balls (radius = an ideal); the exact dense subring is the eventually constant
adeles; Tate's harmonic analysis reduces to finite Fourier transforms on `O_K / a` plus Gaussians; class groups are the
one expensive input; the computable-analysis foundations are unwritten for `A_K`. TJO intends to consider an
implementation on top of FLINT; section 3 of the note lists what FLINT has and lacks (`A_Q` first).
