**Answer to "can that be".** Yes: every quantum expander in the literature and in the book is a
channel on an ungraded bond, its ring zeta has only poles, and on an ungraded bond the periodic and
antiperiodic closures coincide (prop:no-ungraded-zeros). Zeros need a grading and the periodic
closure; they are the eigenvalues on the odd sector (parity coherences) of the doubled bond.

**The definition** (def:graded-transfer-channel, def:graded-rh-fe-ramanujan; note
`notes/ramanujan-graded/definition.md`): on the divisor nu = m_even - m_odd of the periodic ring
zeta 1/sdet(1 - uE); growth q = Perron root; trivial set {q, its period images, 1, FE partners};
critical circle |lambda| = sqrt q (line Re z = omega/2 for generators). RH = one-sided square-root
cancellation; FE = an even similarity J E J^-1 = q E^-1; Ramanujan = both; manifest (Hilbert-Polya)
form = a Gamma_b-even positive form making E/sqrt q unitary on the nontrivial sectors. Stated on the
ring norms so it passes to the continuum verbatim (e^{eps T} has the same ring norms for every eps,
prop:graded-continuum-exact); in a genuine Chernoff limit the grading survives only in the jump
operators, because an odd letter is never close to 1 (prop:graded-chernoff): parity-changing
dynamics is dissipative in the continuum. For infinite bonds the divisor is defined from
correlation functions of regular data, not the L2 spectrum (obs:divisor-regular-data; the
||Z(t)|| = 1 negative of yesterday is the reason).

**Corrected during the session (by me, before the prover):** the first draft put the P-mode
(E(P) = r_P P under parity-twisted trace preservation sum eps_s A_s A_s^+ = r_P 1, a new identity
the 06h elliptic tensor satisfies with r_P = 1) into the trivial set. Wrong: for the Pauli letters

**Graded quantum expanders exist, with a mechanism** (thm:graded-harrow): an irreducible
representation induced from an index-two subgroup is graded by the coset parity (Clifford theory),
Ad(P) is an intertwiner splitting End(V) into the two sectors, the trivial and sign representations
both sit in the even sector (fixed point and P-mode), every other constituent obeys Harrow's bound,
and the graded quantum Ihara-Bass identity (thm:graded-qihara-bass, sector by sector, with
(1-u^2) exponents n_k(D-2)/2 that cancel for balanced gradings) puts the odd edge eigenvalues on
|mu| = sqrt q: zeros on the critical circle. Graded Alon-Boppana (prop:graded-alon-boppana, from
Hastings' lower-bound argument) makes lambda_H optimal for the odd sector too. [Refuted by the prover
lane later the same morning, see below; the row is now obs:odd-gap-no-alon-boppana.]
