# 2026-09-21 — The adelic symplectic space as the bond: the class-group bond, the theta functional as its state, the cusps as the GL_1 bond, Riemann's theta for Q (shard 04p)

TJO restated the genesis of the campaign: attach a symplectic space to `Q^x \ (R_+ x prod_p Z)`, get a
Heisenberg–Weyl representation and hence an automorphism. Four Opus distillation lanes first collected what
the repository had already established (Weil representations and where unitarity came from; Bost–Connes,
adeles and where the zeros sit; the founding transcript's record; the energy-form and metric findings of the
cavity campaign). Reconsideration, in conversation: the naive quotient collapses to `R_+` with commuting
dilations and is the blind side A (`obs:prime-by-prime-blind`; the Weyl-covariance collapse and the symplectic
odd-sector counterexample of shard 04d); the symplectic space lives on the additive side, `A (+) A` with the
rational Lagrangian, the state is the theta functional, the automorphism group is `SL_2(Q)` through the Weil
representation, `Q^x` its torus, the functional equation Poisson summation; unitarity of a metaplectic operator
carries no information (Artin–Schreier: Parseval first, Weil implementer read off after), so the whole problem
is the canonical metric, where the notebook's `thm:arithmetic-metric` says the unitarising cone exists and the
physical metric is outside it. TJO then asked whether it is consistent to take the adelic symplectic space as
the *bond* (auxiliary) system rather than the bulk. Answer: yes, and the bulk reading is exactly the blind one;
the bond reading identifies the bond.

## What was found

- **The class-group bond.** For a function field with a rational point, the units-invariant part of the adelic
  bond is `l^2(Pic^0) (x) l^2(Z)` with the degree shift as transfer; its ring norms are the effective-divisor
  counts `a_n = sum_{Pic^n}(q^{h^0(D)}-1)/(q-1)`, generating `Z(T)`; Riemann–Roch makes `a_n` generic for
  `n > 2g-2` (the Perron pair, even) and the special degrees (the theta divisor) carry `P(T)`, the `H^1` factor.
  At genus one `Z = 1 + hT/((1-T)(1-qT))`, `P = 1 - (q+1-h)T + qT^2`, `h = P(1) = N_1`: the bond dimension is the
  zeta numerator at `T = 1` and fixes the Frobenius roots (D2: `1-2T+2T^2`; D3: `1+3T^2`).
- **The theta functional is the bond state.** `Theta(x 1_{O_A}) = q^{h^0(div x)}`; Tate's integral of the unit ball
  is `Z(T)`; Poisson summation on `A/K` for the unit ball is Riemann–Roch, so the Weyl element (adelic Fourier
  transform) acts on the bond state as `D -> K_C - D`; the two poles are the two constant terms of `Theta`.
- **The cusps are the GL_1 bond.** Cusps of `GL_2(R)\T` are `Pic(R) = Pic^0(K)`: one for D2, four for D3. The
  hedgehog's spikes are `l^2(Pic^0)`; H-CLASS is the Fourier transform on it; the three monomial channels of D3
  are the three nontrivial class characters, whose Hecke `L`-functions are `1` at genus one. `h = 4 = dim K_HW`
  at D3 is an accident (`P(1) = 4g`), not "one cusp per zero mode".
- **Over Q.** Bond `L^2(R_+^x)`, one cusp, bond state Jacobi's theta (`Theta(xf) = theta(x^2)`), even sector the
  constant terms `1, y^{-1/2}` exchanged by `y -> 1/y`, and `int_0^inf (theta - 1 - y^{-1/2}) y^{s/2} d^x y =
  2 pi^{-s/2} Gamma(s/2) zeta(s) = 4 xi(s)/(s(s-1))` in the strip only (the brief's `2 xi/(s(s-1))` and "for all
  `s`" were wrong; numerics F1, F2): Riemann's 1859 argument is the `Q` case. H-THETA (`conj:h-theta`): the
  compressed dilation on the odd theta's cyclic space, outgoing half removed, is the Riemann channel on `K_S`.
- **Not gained: the metric.** At genus one the roots are fixed by `h` and their modulus is Hasse; any
  Frobenius-normal metric is diagonal in the eigenbasis and the symmetries fix its ratios, so the
  symmetric-ray-is-Hodge test is vacuous at genus one (numerics D29, D30); the Kraus dichotomy stands.

## Literature (read directly, TeX sources fetched: `refs/src/0912.3200`, `1307.2494`; `2501.08803` and `1709.06052` were already in the tree)

Three different things are called a square root of Ihara--Bass. (i) Matsuura--Ohta, *Fermions and zeta
function on the graph* (2501.08803, already `cit:mo-dirac` in 08d): a Dirac operator from deformed incidence
matrices, block off-diagonal between vertices and the two edge blocks, plus a mass term; one determinant,
two Schur evaluations, Bass's and Hashimoto's; `(1-t^2)^{n_E-n_V}` from `det(I - tJ)`. (ii) Loebl--Somberg,
*Discrete Dirac operators, critical embeddings and Ihara--Selberg functions* (0912.3200): the Ising
partition function is a linear combination of the square roots of `2^{2g}` Ihara--Selberg functions with
spin-structure-twisted transition matrices, and those square roots are discrete-Dirac Pfaffians; Cimasoni
(1307.2494): the Kac--Ward determinant is the square of a polynomial; Aizenman--Warzel (1709.06052, Lemma
3.4): non-backtracking + loopwise time-reversal invariant + twist anti-symmetric scalar flow matrix gives a
polynomial square, each oriented prime loop the square of its unoriented class. (iii) The notebook's own:
bipartite pairing `z <-> -z`, resonances as square roots of Frobenius eigenvalues, `Z^2 ~ Fr^{-1}` (04k/04n).
The ideation ledger's item 32 (redo quantum Ihara--Bass as a Berezin integral over a chiral Dirac operator so
`chi` is an index) and L08-022 (chirality versus flow superparity) were the open loose ends.

**What was found.**
- *Chiral linearisation* (T1). The pencil `N(u) = [[1 + uJ, S],[uR, 1]]` on `W (+) V` has `det N = det(1 - uH)`
  and `= det(1 + uJ) det(1 - uR(1+uJ)^{-1}S)`: `thm:qihara-general` is one determinant evaluated twice. The
  nonzero spectra of `XY` (on `V`) and `YX = u(H+J)(1+uJ)^{-1}` (on `W`) coincide, the paired even operator
  `K = YX (+) XY` on `W_even (+) V_odd` has `str K^k = 0` and `sdet(1 - K) = 1`; the index `N(D-1)` is a
  difference of generalized-kernel dimensions (prover's counterexample for ordinary kernels). The Euler
  exponent `N(D-2)/2` is the Berezinian of the *rescaled* diagonal mass `diag(1 + uJ, (1-u^2)1_V)` with
  edges even and vertices odd, the parity `k+1` of `def:graded-geodesic-determinant` extended to `k = 0`.
  The coupling is not a Berezinian: the two Schur values `p` and `b^2/p` differ already for one loop; the
  scalar off-diagonal blocks are not odd, so the even-Gaussian Berezinian formula does not apply (the brief's
  "the integral is not defined" was an overclaim, corrected). Ledger item 32 is closed: the index is the
  mass Berezinian; the coupling stays an ordinary determinant, as in Matsuura--Ohta.
- *Oriented half* (T2). No primitive cyclically non-backtracking class is self-reverse (all `l`, including
  the even case). For adjoint-paired letters `det(1 - uH) = F(u) conj(F(conj u))` in the disc
  `|u| < 1/((D-1) max||E_i||)` (prover's radius, better than the brief's), `= |F|^2 > 0` on the real interval;
  **for Kraus letters `Ad(A)` preserves Hermitian matrices, `det(1 - z E_w)` has real coefficients and
  `det(1 - uH) = F(u)^2` on the whole disc** (prover's strengthening, confirmed numerically at complex `u`);
  the non-Kraus pair `E_1 = i = -E_2` shows the general case is only `F F^#`. `F` is not a polynomial
  (two-loop bouquet, simple roots at `-1`, `1/3`). The brief's claim that Ad-weights are twist *symmetric*
  was wrong in the transition convention (prover's `U_e = U_f = diag(1,i)`: path traces `0` and `4`); my
  first numerics had tested the endpoint-inclusive identity instead; corrected. Aizenman--Warzel's lemma
  needs scalar weights and twist anti-symmetry; Ad path traces are nonnegative, so it cannot apply; the
  channel's square root is the power-series one, an MPS amplitude per prime cycle.
- *Odd letters* (T3, T4). For all-odd unitary letters (index-two graded channels; this forces `D_+ = D_-`) the chirality
  `Gamma_c = L_P (x) 1` (left multiplication by the bond parity) anticommutes with `H`, `J`, `Sigma` and each
  sector; in general it conjugates a graded channel to its sign-character twist. Consequences: symmetric
  sector spectra, `det(1 - uH_k) = det_+(1 - u^2 K_k)` with `K_k = H_k^2|_+`, the graded zeta a rational
  function of `u^2` and the square root of the zeta of the even two-step channel; `Sigma_k = [[0, a],[a^*, 0]]`,
  the Bass quadratic `det_+((1 + qu^2)^2 - u^2 a a^*)`, and the Ramanujan band `<=>` `a a^* <= 4q`: the graded
  Ramanujan property is a bound on the even Laplacian block. Zeros: `W_1^+ = Hom(V_-, V_+) (x) C^D`, each sign
  pair of odd edge eigenvalues is one eigenvalue of `K_1`, and **the order of a candidate zero is
  `b_1(rho) - b_0(rho)`** (prover: four letters `X` give `Z_gr = 1` with `K_1 != 0`); the entire-sector circle
  condition is the modulus-`q` condition on `K_1`, stronger than the divisor RH (a degree-six tensor with
  modes `+-5` outside the band and `Z_gr = 1`); **the manifest Hilbert--Polya form needs semisimplicity**
  (prover's refutation of the brief's "iff diagonalisable": `U_0, U_0, U_{pi/6}, U_{pi/6}` on `C^{1|1}`, band
  edge, `det(1 - uH_1) = (1-u^2)^2(1-3u^2)^2`, Jordan block of `K_1` at 3). The Perron mode and the `P`-mode
  are one chiral pair. One even letter breaks anticommutation with `L_P` (Pauli `{6, -2}`), but another
  chiral involution can exist (prover's `L_{1 (x) Z}`); in the continuum a jump Lindbladian with
  `sum L_i^* L_i = c 1` keeps the reflection about `-c` (brief's "no continuum analogue" corrected). Single
  layer: `h` is odd, `str h^k = 0`, the supertrace series is 1 (no Berezinian of `1 - uh`); the induction
  identity `L(u, pi) = L(u, pi_+; Y)` proved directly (no H-ARTIN); the doubled supertrace is the squared norm
  of the amplitude vector; `X, X, Y, Y`: `Z_gr = (1+3u^2)^2/((1-u^2)(1-9u^2))` against the single-layer
  `(1-u^2)^2(1-2u^2+9u^4)`, no spectral-squaring identification. Prover: 24 correction-ledger rows, all
  applied; review 10 VALID / 0 MINOR / 0 INVALID after the corrections; 158 checks.
