## What was found

- **The class-group bond.** For a function field with a rational point, the units-invariant part of the adelic
  bond is `l^2(Pic^0) (x) l^2(Z)` with the degree shift as transfer; its ring norms are the effective-divisor
  counts `a_n = sum_{Pic^n}(q^{h^0(D)}-1)/(q-1)`, generating `Z(T)`; Riemann–Roch makes `a_n` generic for
  `n > 2g-2` (the Perron pair, even) and the special degrees (the theta divisor) carry `P(T)`, the `H^1` factor.
  At genus one `Z = 1 + hT/((1-T)(1-qT))`, `P = 1 - (q+1-h)T + qT^2`, `h = P(1) = N_1`: the bond dimension is the
  zeta numerator at `T = 1` and fixes the Frobenius roots (D2: `1-2T+2T^2`; D3: `1+3T^2`).
- **The theta functional is the bond state.** `Theta(x 1_{O_A}) = q^{h^0(div x)}`; Tate's integral of the unit ball
  is `Z(T)`; Poisson summation on `A/K` for the unit ball is Riemann–Roch, so the Weyl element (adelic Fourier
  transform) acts on the bond state as `D -> K_C - D`; the two poles are the two constant terms of `Theta`.
- **The cusps are the GL_1 bond.** Cusps of `GL_2(R)\T` are `Pic(R) = Pic^0(K)`: one for D2, four for D3. The
  hedgehog's spikes are `l^2(Pic^0)`; H-CLASS is the Fourier transform on it; the three monomial channels of D3
  are the three nontrivial class characters, whose Hecke `L`-functions are `1` at genus one. `h = 4 = dim K_HW`
  at D3 is an accident (`P(1) = 4g`), not "one cusp per zero mode".
- **Over Q.** Bond `L^2(R_+^x)`, one cusp, bond state Jacobi's theta (`Theta(xf) = theta(x^2)`), even sector the
  constant terms `1, y^{-1/2}` exchanged by `y -> 1/y`, and `int_0^inf (theta - 1 - y^{-1/2}) y^{s/2} d^x y =
  2 pi^{-s/2} Gamma(s/2) zeta(s) = 4 xi(s)/(s(s-1))` in the strip only (the brief's `2 xi/(s(s-1))` and "for all
  `s`" were wrong; numerics F1, F2): Riemann's 1859 argument is the `Q` case. H-THETA (`conj:h-theta`): the
  compressed dilation on the odd theta's cyclic space, outgoing half removed, is the Riemann channel on `K_S`.
- **Not gained: the metric.** At genus one the roots are fixed by `h` and their modulus is Hasse; any
  Frobenius-normal metric is diagonal in the eigenbasis and the symmetries fix its ratios, so the
  symmetric-ray-is-Hodge test is vacuous at genus one (numerics D29, D30); the Kraus dichotomy stands.

## The maths worked out before dispatch (brief `notes/h-theta/astra-brief.md`, author claude:fable-5.1)

The odd part `phi = theta - 1 - y^{-1/2}` satisfies `phi(1/y) = y^{1/2} phi(y)` exactly; the weight `y^{1/4}` is the
only one making `g = y^{1/4} phi` square-integrable and inversion-invariant, and it is Connes's `|x|^{1/2}` (Burnol's
subtracted `E`). Its Mellin transform is `-xi(1/2 + 2i tau)/(tau^2 + 1/16)`, decaying like `e^{-pi|tau|/2}`, so it
violates the Szegő condition and its forward dilation orbit is dense: the literal H-THETA is degenerate. The
correct GL_1 statement is Burnol's causality theorem (2000): a Lax–Phillips system on the GL_1 bond whose outgoing
subspace is the orthocomplement of the theta images of unit-ball test functions satisfies every axiom except
causality, and causality is equivalent to RH. And the GL_2 symbol is a ratio of two GL_1 theta channels:
`Theta(tau) = [Lambda(1+2i tau)/Lambda(2i tau)] (tau - i/2)/(tau + i/2)`, the odd theta read on the two edge lines of
the strip, which are exactly the two constant-term coefficients of the completed Eisenstein series (Mellin transform
of the Siegel theta). Six identities checked with mpmath before dispatch. Sources fetched: Burnol math/0001013,
0203120, 0112254, 0407443, 0602425, 9911175, 9809119, 0208121; Meyer math/0311468; Uetake 2007 (Ann. Polon. Math.
92.2, pdftotext, `refs/src/uetake-2007/`).

## Literature (read directly, TeX sources fetched: `refs/src/0912.3200`, `1307.2494`; `2501.08803` and `1709.06052` were already in the tree)

Three different things are called a square root of Ihara--Bass. (i) Matsuura--Ohta, *Fermions and zeta
function on the graph* (2501.08803, already `cit:mo-dirac` in 08d): a Dirac operator from deformed incidence
matrices, block off-diagonal between vertices and the two edge blocks, plus a mass term; one determinant,
two Schur evaluations, Bass's and Hashimoto's; `(1-t^2)^{n_E-n_V}` from `det(I - tJ)`. (ii) Loebl--Somberg,
*Discrete Dirac operators, critical embeddings and Ihara--Selberg functions* (0912.3200): the Ising
partition function is a linear combination of the square roots of `2^{2g}` Ihara--Selberg functions with
spin-structure-twisted transition matrices, and those square roots are discrete-Dirac Pfaffians; Cimasoni
(1307.2494): the Kac--Ward determinant is the square of a polynomial; Aizenman--Warzel (1709.06052, Lemma
3.4): non-backtracking + loopwise time-reversal invariant + twist anti-symmetric scalar flow matrix gives a
polynomial square, each oriented prime loop the square of its unoriented class. (iii) The notebook's own:
bipartite pairing `z <-> -z`, resonances as square roots of Frobenius eigenvalues, `Z^2 ~ Fr^{-1}` (04k/04n).
