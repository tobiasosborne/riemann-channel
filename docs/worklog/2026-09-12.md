## Circle flows and the prime side (discussion, no code)

TJO proposed taking, as Lindblad generator, the translation generator on
a circle of circumference $\log p$, summed over primes. Worked out in
conversation: Poisson summation gives $\operatorname{Tr} U_s = L\sum_k
\delta(s - kL)$, so the direct sum over primes has trace
$\sum_n \Lambda(n)\,\delta(s - \log n)$ with the von Mangoldt weights
coming out of the circumference automatically, and its dynamical zeta is
the Euler product. This is side A, the permutation, not the transfer
matrix: for any finite set $S$ of primes the partial Euler product's poles
are exactly the rotation frequencies $(2\pi i/\log p)\mathbb Z$, so the
zeros appear only in the infinite product. The explicit formula written
as a trace identity, for $s>0$ after damping by $e^{-s/2}$:
circles damped at rate $1/2$ plus the even part of the dilation flow on
$\mathbb R$ equals $e^{s/2} + e^{-s/2}$ minus the trace over the zeros.
Two remarks kept: $L_1 - L_{-1}$ in the Witt convention is the dilation
flow (source and sink), $L_0$ is the rotation; and any prime-by-prime
ansatz (direct sums, product states, commuting dilations) has the
finite-$S$ spectrum and is blind to zeros. The only relation between
primes is additive.

## Afternoon: prior art for the quantum Ihara zeta, and the general Kraus formula

TJO asked whether the quantum Ihara construction was new. Three Opus
literature agents (graph-zeta / L-functions; quantum expanders and free
probability; direct phrase search), then every load-bearing claim re-checked
against arXiv **TeX sources** (`refs/`, 16 papers, sha256 manifest; TJO's
standing preference from now on: TeX source, not PDF). Result, in
`notes/prior-art-quantum-ihara.md`: the matrix-weighted Ihara zeta with
adjoint weights $U_e\otimes U_e^\dagger$ and its Bass formula are in
Matsuura–Ohta (JHEP 2022, arXiv:2204.06424, eqs. 2.8–2.11), with the
Artin–Ihara $L$-function reading in their arXiv:2607.27935; the
arbitrary-weight Bass formula for loopless graphs is Watanabe–Fukumizu
(JMLR 2011); representation-twisted zetas go back to Sunada 1986. Not found
anywhere: a zeta attached to a quantum channel, "RH ⇔ Ramanujan quantum
expander", or the MPS/ring-norm reading. Exactly Ramanujan channels from LPS
generators via Harrow's theorem are stated by Iyer–Jain–Jordan–Somma
(arXiv:2602.15180, Feb 2026) with $SU(2)$ irreps; the Weil-representation
instance is not in the literature.

First result then written and proved: the Ihara–Bass formula for the
non-backtracking superoperator of an **arbitrary** Kraus family (no
unitarity, no invertibility), `notes/quantum-ihara-general.md`, Lamport
structured proof, rk-light status tags. The $(D-1)u^2$ becomes an operator
$\mathcal D(u)$ and $u\,D\Phi$ becomes a deformed channel $\mathcal A(u)$,
both built from $(1-u^2\operatorname{Ad}(A_k^\dagger A_k))^{-1}$; the
unitary formula is Corollary 3; side A is positive for every Kraus family,
$\operatorname{Tr}T^\ell = \sum_w|\operatorname{Tr}A_w|^2$. Numerics
(`scripts/qihara_general.py`): float to $10^{-15}$ in five configurations,
exact sympy for $(N,D) = (1,4),(2,4),(2,6)$. Adversarial review, round 1,
two Opus reviewers (`notes/reviews/`): algebra VALID with four cosmetic
fixes (applied); provenance found no misquote but 15 wrong file names or

one framing error: Watanabe–Fukumizu's graphs are loopless, so the bouquet
formula is an extension of their corollary, not an instance. All applied.
Round 2 (one Opus reviewer): every fix confirmed, 48/48 citations
