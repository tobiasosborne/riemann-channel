## Circle flows and the prime side (discussion, no code)

TJO proposed taking, as Lindblad generator, the translation generator on
a circle of circumference $\log p$, summed over primes. Worked out in
conversation: Poisson summation gives $\operatorname{Tr} U_s = L\sum_k
\delta(s - kL)$, so the direct sum over primes has trace
$\sum_n \Lambda(n)\,\delta(s - \log n)$ with the von Mangoldt weights
coming out of the circumference automatically, and its dynamical zeta is
the Euler product. This is side A, the permutation, not the transfer
matrix: for any finite set $S$ of primes the partial Euler product's poles
are exactly the rotation frequencies $(2\pi i/\log p)\mathbb Z$, so the
zeros appear only in the infinite product. The explicit formula written
as a trace identity, for $s>0$ after damping by $e^{-s/2}$:
circles damped at rate $1/2$ plus the even part of the dilation flow on
$\mathbb R$ equals $e^{s/2} + e^{-s/2}$ minus the trace over the zeros.
Two remarks kept: $L_1 - L_{-1}$ in the Witt convention is the dilation
flow (source and sink), $L_0$ is the rotation; and any prime-by-prime
ansatz (direct sums, product states, commuting dilations) has the
finite-$S$ spectrum and is blind to zeros. The only relation between
primes is additive.

## Siemon–Holevo–Werner, unbounded generators (TJO's pointer, arXiv:1707.02266)

TeX fetched (`refs/src/1707.02266`, manifest updated), sixteen byte-verified
quotes in `notes/extract/shw-unbounded-generators-sources.md`. Assessment
against the notebook, recorded for HANDOFF step 2:

1. The Riemann channel $\rho\mapsto Z(t)^*\rho Z(t)$ on $B(K_S)$ is a
   *no-event semigroup* in their sense (pure states to multiples of pure
   states; necessarily $C_t\rho C_t^*$ with $C_t$ a contraction semigroup).
   Hence it has no jump operators at all, and `conj:stinespring-jump-form`
   read literally (a Stinespring form of $Z(t)$ with prime dilations as
   jumps) is answered negatively with a reason: any generator with nonzero
   jump term produces mixed states at first order.
2. Its *exit space* $(\mathcal E, j)$, $\langle j\psi,j\phi\rangle =
   -\langle (B+B^*)\psi,\phi\rangle$, is one-dimensional for a scalar inner
   function (rank-one dissipative part; Livšic), i.e. the cusp; for $\Gamma(N)$
   with $h$ cusps it is $\mathbb C^h$. The map $J\psi(t) = jZ(t)\psi$ is an
   isometry $K_S\to L^2(\mathbb R_+;\mathcal E)$ because $Z(t)\to0$ strongly:
   this is the Lax–Phillips outgoing translation representation restricted
   to $K_S$, read as the arrival-time wavefunction at the cusp. Their
   half-sided-shift example (Sect. "Non-closable jump operators") is exactly
   this structure in the translation picture: exit through $\psi(0)$,
   rebound state $\Omega$, jump operators $|\phi_\alpha\rangle\langle j|$
   that are not closable.
3. Standard generators whose no-event part is $Z$ are therefore
   parametrised by a single subnormalised rebound state $\Omega$ on $K_S$
   (their Prop. on exit spaces, item (a)): $\mathcal L\rho = B\rho + \rho B^*
   + \langle j,\rho j\rangle\,\Omega$, conservative iff $\operatorname{Tr}\Omega
   = 1$, a renewal process "exit at the cusp, reinsert at $\Omega$". Its
   spectrum is governed by $1 = \hat m_\Omega(\lambda)$ with $m_\Omega$ the
   cusp arrival density from $\Omega$. This is the "more intricate
   semigroup" their framework hands us for free; whether its spectral
   problem sees RH is open.
4. Their gauge lemma (sign as in its proof, not as displayed; noted in the
   extract) moves the primes between no-event part and jumps: in the
   compensated gauge $\lambda_p = -\sqrt{r_p}$ the Bost–Connes Holevo
   generator at $\sigma>1$ becomes a no-event generator
   $K' = i\,\mathrm{drift} - \sum_p p^{-\sigma}(1-U_p)$ whose symbol is the
   Lévy exponent, loss form $2\log|\zeta(\sigma)/\zeta(\sigma-i\tau)|$,
   plus compensated jumps $\sqrt{r_p}(U_p-1)$. The shard-04 bridge "drop
   the divergent real part, keep the phase" is: subtract the divergent
   constant $\log\zeta(\sigma)$ from this loss form at $\sigma\to1$; the
   finite remainder $-\log|\zeta(1-i\tau)|$ is indefinite, so it is not a
   dissipative generator, and the inner-function construction replaces it
   by a contraction with the same phase. That is a precise statement of
   what the Sz.-Nagy–Foias step does and does not preserve.
5. The Weil-positivity Dyson formula (thm:cmps-ring-norms) is the trace
   over events of their iterated dilation $J^{(n)}_\tau$ with free
   propagators between jumps; their remark that once $J$ is bounded no
   further domain questions arise is the route to the unbounded version of
   the continuous ring norms.
6. Non-standard generators (domain with no new pure states) come from
   explosion plus reinsertion at infinity. The arithmetic place where the
   no-event loss rate diverges is the infinite-activity Bost–Connes regime
   $\sigma\le1$ (total jump rate $\log\zeta(\sigma)$); a conservative
   completion of the critical process by reinsertion at the cusp is the
   candidate non-standard object. Idea only; nothing computed.

"environment is the cusp" (shard 04) is now literally the exit space.
