## What was found

- **The Tate-twist identity (R1).** Deninger's Lefschetz formula for `Q` on `R^{>0}` is `prop:ringnorm-trace`
  under `lambda -> (lambda - 1)/2` (the twist `H(1)`, `t = 2 t_D`) with the archimedean term moved to the operator
  side and counted odd; verified by the reviewer to `1e-15` with 3000 zeros. The difference of the divisors of
  `zeta-hat` and `zeta` is that of `Gamma_R`, which is Deninger's `R_infty = R[exp(-2y)]`: the notebook's odd
  ladder is the trivial zeros, and the parity freedom of `prop:ladder-parity-free` is the side of the trace
  formula that carries the archimedean place. The notebook's even line is his `H^2`, not `H^0`.
- **Suspension = ring (R2).** A one-prime suspension is the periodic MPS ring of length `log p`; the zeros are joint
  eigenvalues of commuting Frobenii, the towers collapsing only for a non-product leaf space; the lifted
  Frobenius is a covering, not a diffeomorphism, so Deninger's solenoid is needed (reviewer). The Bost–Connes
  vectors `sum n^{-rho} e_n` are joint eigenvectors for every `Re rho > 1/2`; nothing in `l^2(N)` selects zeros.
- **A rotation angle per prime (R3).** Deninger's orbit coefficient is the sign of the graded trace of the exterior
  algebra of the leaf plane; conformality plus `epsilon = +1` makes the return map `sqrt p` times an `SO(2)`
  rotation, with an angle the Ruelle zeta does not see; an integer trace gives an elliptic curve (Deuring), a
  higher-degree Weil number an abelian variety (reviewer's `x^4 + x^2 + 4`).
- **The metric as an invariant polarisation (R4).** The cup product gives `Theta^# = c - Theta` (FE); a Hodge
  star commuting with the flow gives (HP) outright, not the Kraus dichotomy's case (d) where Hastings' bound
  remains (reviewer). The invariant polarisation is unique for simple spectrum, `U(p,q)/(U(p) x U(q))` for a
  repeated eigenvalue of Krein signature `(p,q)` (reviewer's count; version 1's degenerate clause was false), and
  invariant symplectic forms have the same dimension as invariant metrics, so `B` buys uniqueness only when
  canonical. On `K_HW` of D2 no cup form is defined; the reviewer's eigen-model shows every `B`-compatible
  polarisation lies in the cone of `thm:arithmetic-metric` and on its symmetric ray iff `B` is
  parity-(anti)invariant.
- **Compact manifolds, solenoids (R5).** The invariant volume `vol_F wedge omega_phi` forces `c = 0`; the elliptic
  solenoid evades it by a contracting transverse measure, with the gluing a `g`-isometry and the return map the
  contraction (reviewer's wording fix). The adelic objects of 04p are not suspensions of this type.
- **The elliptic example is the Hodge ket bond (R6).** Deninger's `(H^*(C/Gamma), pi^*, (-1)^deg)` is
  `thm:hodge-doubling-tensor`'s doubled bond; his Hodge metric is the doubled ket-bond metric; conformality is
  `thm:elliptic-ph-unitary`. His and the notebook's reductions exchange their free and missing inputs.
- **`K_S` is not Deninger's `H^1` (R7, VALID at first pass).** The zero-mode kernels are not a Riesz basis
  (Carleson fails; consecutive modes have pseudo-hyperbolic distance at most the zero gap), so no inner product
  equivalent to the Hardy one makes `Z(t)` normal with the modes as eigenvectors; the two-point bound
  `sigma_min <= 1 - sqrt(1 - rho_min^2)`; `rho_min = 0.097` and `sigma_min` down to `0.016` at `N = 400` on the
  first 3000 zeros, reproduced by the reviewer to three digits and continued to `N = 1600`.
- **The bond as an algebra (R8, INVALID at first pass, corrected).** Version 1 claimed the derivation mechanism
  acts only on scalar-times-automorphism channels; false, the relevant product is the exterior product of the
  doubled bond, for which the genus-one Hodge ket transfer is a ring endomorphism (automorphism iff `a != 0`)
  although the ket letter is not a scalar times a unitary. Whether any other notebook bond carries such a
  product is open.
- **Verdict.** A reformulation with the same gap. Deninger's own words: the conformal metric will not exist for
  number fields and the Kähler identities on cohomology are needed (`math/0505354:674`; Serre's Kähler analogue,
  Leichtnam `:423`); the lifted elliptic curve is misleading (`:1230`); the 2018 spaces have no analytic
  structure (`1807.06400:559`), map into Connes's adele space (`:2363`, reviewer), and get `H^0 = R` from an
  irreducible leaf space (`:3021-3023`, reviewer). His second mechanism, a Laplacian with `-(Theta - 1/2)^2` as
  restriction (`:852-869`, reviewer), has the same equivariance gap and a `3g`-dimensional metric cone.
