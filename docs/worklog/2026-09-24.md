## Afternoon: the finite-prime language and the FNW exclusion argument (future direction, record only)

TJO: build a language (complexity class) of all quantities obtainable by finite computations using finitely
many primes; argue some classes of numbers are inside and some outside (MIP* = RE style); find a value of zeta
outside it. Precedent: Fannes–Nachtergaele–Werner, "finite bond dim FCS can only produce non-transcendental
energy densities", so the Heisenberg ground state (Hulthén energy `1/4 - log 2`) is not finitely correlated.
Recorded in `notes/metric-tomography/finite-prime-language.md`. FNW 1992 could not be byte-cited (Springer,
Project Euclid, academia.edu refuse the proxy; no open copy); the precedent stands as TJO's statement.

- **Rigorous form of the precedent (Proposition A).** The one-liner is about the optimum, not individual
  states (FCS with transcendental entries have transcendental energies). For fixed bond dimension the FCS
  class is semialgebraic over the real algebraic numbers and the energy density is semialgebraic, so
  `inf_{F_d} e` is a real algebraic number (Tarski–Seidenberg); a transcendental ground-state energy excludes
  every finite `d`, strictly. Three ingredients: a finitely parametrised semialgebraic class, a target known
  transcendental, the target defined implicitly with an independent exact computation. Quantitative remark:
  effective quantifier elimination plus a transcendence measure for `log 2` gives a rigorous lower bound on
  matrix-product convergence for the Heisenberg chain (untested, small project).
- **The language made precise.** `L_S = Q-bar`-span of `1`, `log p` (`p in S`) and the archimedean values
  (`gamma`, `pi`, logs of algebraic numbers, polygamma at rationals). Baker: the prime part is a direct sum,
  one direction per prime, which is the prime-content filtration of the tomography note in number-theoretic
  form. Gauss's digamma theorem: `psi(rational)` adds only `gamma`. Polygamma at 1 gives every `zeta(n)`:
  the archimedean place is the additive structure and produces the integer zeta values by itself.
- **Central negative finding.** Nothing of interest is provably outside `L_S`: no zero ordinate is known
  irrational, `lambda_n` for `n >= 2` involve Stieltjes-type constants of open status, `zeta(2n)` and
  `zeta(3)` are inside through `Gamma`, `pi^2` versus logarithms needs Schanuel. The known transcendentals
  (`log p`, `zeta(2n)`) are each produced by a single place and so, by `obs:prime-by-prime-blind`, see no
  zero. The FNW route for the *metric* (where the target is implicit and finite-window classes are
  semialgebraic) is blocked on exactly one open input: transcendence or irrationality of `gamma`, a Stieltjes
  constant, or a zero ordinate.
- **MIP* = RE.** Exact at the level `Pi^0_1` (Davis–Matiyasevich–Robinson, Lagarias's inequality; the Weil
  violation certificate is `Sigma^0_1`) and empty at the level of uncomputability (single sentence, all
  quantities computable). A tensor-versus-commuting gap cannot occur in the window channel (finite-atom
  extensions, `prop:extension-disc`(iii)); it can only live on the noncommutative adelic Weyl bond of
  shard 04p, as "the arithmetic state is not a weak-* limit of kinematically constrained finite-bond states".
