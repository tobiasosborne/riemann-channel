## Weil numerator as ring norm (discussion)

TJO asked whether the numerator/denominator of an elliptic-curve zeta should be
realised by boson–fermion MPS ring norms. Yes via the P-closed (periodic
fermion) ring: minimal bond C^{1|1} realises N_n = |1 - alpha^n|^2 even with

|alpha| <= sqrt q for free, the functional equation is saturation. Circular
unless the tensor comes from the curve; genus >= 2 needs structure. Details in

## Night: back to basics, the graded permutation (shard 03b)

TJO: "I want to go right back to basics. Let us consider a permutation, e.g. (1 2)(3 4 5)."
Three questions in sequence, answered in discussion and then recorded at TJO's request
("this seems important. please record it durably, also in the lab book"):

1. **Rings, gas, oscillators.** Point-letter ring norm = number of periodic points =
   Lambda_sigma(n) (the weight "log p" is the number of starting points on the prime ring);
   the gas of rings is the Fock space of one oscillator per cycle by unique factorisation,
   H = 2 N_2 + 3 N_3, zeta = Tr e^{-beta H}; the same number is the twisted Fock trace
   Tr u^N Gamma(P) over the five-oscillator bond space (fixed monomials = gas configurations);
   the square-root-weight vector is a product state whose norm is zeta, Gibbs only on the
   diagonal algebra (the full Gibbs state needs the ket/bra doubling).
2. **Grading (3 4 5) odd.** Ramond closure: str P^n = 2[2|n] - 3[3|n], zeta (1-u^3)/(1-u^2),
   zeros at the primitive cube roots, the pole at u = 1 eaten by the odd fixed vector (rigidity
   in miniature); a holonomy on the odd cycle moves the zeros (h = -1 keeps the pole);
   untwisted closure has no zeros; a fermionic prime is a two-level system contributing 1 - u^l
   for every l (all primes fermionic: 1/zeta, Moebius = fermion parity, zeros on Re s = 0).
3. **Physical letters.** With point letters the Ramond NORM is still Tr P^n (signs square);
   the sign lives only in the even-odd coherences of the doubled bond. Ladder at n = 6:
   one letter (2-3)^2 = 1, one letter per cycle 2^2 + 3^2 = 13, one letter per point 5.
   Principle: physical dimension >= Kraus rank of the transfer channel (minimal choice
   canonical up to a unitary; zeta blind above it) and letters must be coarser than the
   grading; 0/1 letters force one letter, weighted letters (sqrt q, phases) allow several.
   Added: a parity jump sqrt(gamma) Pi shifts every odd eigenvalue by -2 gamma and no even one
   (checked on a random graded Lindbladian), the letter-level form of the uniform-rate statement.
4. **TJO's follow-up (mid-recording): prime jumps vs Bost-Connes symmetry jumps for the
   Riemann cMPS.** Reading recorded as obs:jump-operator-guidance: jumps matter only through
   the Lindbladian; Galois jumps are character-diagonal, hence character dephasing = the
   uniform rate (e^{-t/4}), no frequencies, and as a full per-step average a character
   measurement that kills the zeros; prime jumps act on every character sector and carry the
   lengths (the record), alone real spectrum; the grading must be transverse to the primes;
   the frequencies need an even-odd Hamiltonian that neither family supplies; ansatz shape
   prime jumps + Galois dephasing at 2 gamma = 1/4 + even-odd Hamiltonian.
