## Weil numerator as ring norm (discussion)

TJO asked whether the numerator/denominator of an elliptic-curve zeta should be
realised by boson–fermion MPS ring norms. Yes via the P-closed (periodic
fermion) ring: minimal bond C^{1|1} realises N_n = |1 - alpha^n|^2 even with
bosonic tensors, which is Hasse's deg(1 - phi^n); Cauchy–Schwarz gives
|alpha| <= sqrt q for free, the functional equation is saturation. Circular
unless the tensor comes from the curve; genus >= 2 needs structure. Details in
HANDOFF.
