- The spine: the Weil/Toeplitz form is the Gram matrix of open transfer strips in a metric that makes the retained
  transfer unitary (`prop:ccm-tn-operator-gram`); the metric exists iff semisimple on the circle
  (`prop:hp-inner-product-discrete`); positivity alone is the one-sided bound (`thm:weil-positivity-finite`), the
  reflection symmetry makes it the circle (`thm:weil-duality-pairing`). Examples: permutation (native metric),
  shift (the pole; golden mean passes positivity and fails RH), graph (edge reversal = functional equation; Ramanujan
  = Weil positivity, the Hilbert–Pólya metric needing semisimplicity as well), Kraus family (inverse pairing buys duality, adjoint pairing reality, unitary both:
  Hastings), Artin–Schreier (EE† = q, RH manifest), zeta (strips known from the explicit formula, metric unknown).
- Worker corrections absorbed into the prose: the Artin–Schreier sign law is `thm:as-sign-law` (the founding
  note's −(−1)^m only when P_g(−1) ≠ 0); a perturbed transfer matrix need not give a negative form (one-sided
  positivity; the counts stop being integers); with the bipartite trivial set the prism C₁₆×K₂ turns negative at
  K = 14 (the −60.46 at K = 12 came from keeping {−q, −1}); Huang's literal formula needs
  N_k = Tr B^k − (|E|−|V|)(1+(−1)^k) and holds for non-bipartite graphs; charpoly+Durand–Kerner is unreliable for
  repeated eigenvalues at dimension 25 (the AS worker used a small QR).
- Review corrections worth remembering: Ramanujan is Weil positivity, but the Hilbert–Pólya metric on the retained edge space needs semisimplicity too, which fails exactly at a = ±2√q (the reviewer's counterexamples: the 5-regular C₃×C₆×K₂ has 2×2 Jordan blocks of B at ±2 with a positive Weil form; a Z₂₄ unitary family meeting Hastings' bound with equality has Jordan blocks of T at √3); Ad(P_i) of permutation matrices lifts the graph on ordered pairs of vertices (ring norms Σ(#Fix P_w)²), the graph itself is Theorem 1 with E_i = P_i; with point letters the parity-twisted ring norm is still Tr P^k (lem:sector-separating-letters), the sign is in the bond supertrace; −E is the Frobenius matrix only when P_g(−1) ≠ 0 (thm:as-frobenius-block).
