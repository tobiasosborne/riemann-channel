## Overnight: the `zst` benchmark, and what it broke

TJO (evening of the 17th): "Now the code is there how far can we reasonably go beyond the paper? I am
curious how many zeros we can reach and how accurately. Some kind of impromptu benchmark would be
interesting." Orchestrator alone. Write-up: `notes/zeta-spectral-triples/benchmark.md`; raw outputs
`notes/zeta-spectral-triples/bench/`; tools `zst/tools/bench.sh`, `zst/tools/bench_summary.py`.

Grid `x = lambda^2` in {13, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 80, 100}, `N ~ 10 x`, `prec ~ 40 x + 200` bits, plus
N-scans at x = 20 and 30. Every row is certified (Krawczyk eigenpair, positive-definiteness
certificate for even-simplicity, interval-Newton roots complete by count). Results through x = 50 as
of this writing (x = 60, 80, 100 running): first zero to 2.4e-55 (x = 13), 1.2e-92 (20), 2.6e-146 (30),
3.0e-200 (40), 2.9e-249 (50); zeros certified below 1e-3: 47, 95, 172, 251, 315; the accuracy of the
first zero is exponential in x (5.4 digits per unit, `e^{-4 pi x}`) and independent of N, the error
at height gamma grows like `10^{0.37 gamma}` at every x, and N saturates at about 7.5 x.

Four defects surfaced only at scale, each found by the benchmark and fixed test-first:

1. The interval-`LDL^T` inertia certificate (even-simple hypothesis) failed from x = 20: pivot
   radii grow like the squared condition number. Replaced by verified positive definiteness of the
   deflated matrix `E - 2 eps + c v v^T` and of `O - 2 eps` (approximate Cholesky plus a ball residual,
   Rump's isspd; amplification linear in the condition number). The 2x2 unit test caught that the
   deflation constant must exceed `s - eps`.
2. The QR candidate generator dominated the runtime (612 of 673 s at x = 40) and cannot run below the
   dynamic range of the rank-one term (about 5.5 x digits). Replaced by a sign scan with bisection on
   each pole interval plus a geometric grid beyond the last pole; QR kept as a fallback at prec/2
   and prec. Two iterations on the tail grid: the tail roots cluster just past N (400.34, 406.74 at
   N = 400) and come in pairs separated by about a tenth of their distance from N, so the grid starts
   at spacing 1/16 with 64 points per octave. x = 40 now takes 119 s instead of 673 s, identical table.
3. Overlapping certified balls (duplicate candidates) used to discard the entire list. Now a Newton
   step on the hull of the pair decides rigorously whether it is one root or two.
4. At x = 50, N >= 400 the far-out roots (beyond the accurate range) failed interval-Newton
   verification: the plain ball evaluation of h' over the Newton box carries the full dependency
   error, radius (sum |xi_k|) r ~ 1e135 r, so the Newton image has radius |h(m)| 1e135 r / h'^2 and
   cannot fit in the box once |h(m)| is floored by the xi radii (1e-126). A mean-value form
   `h'(m) + h''(X)(X - m)` removes the first-order dependency; x = 50, N = 400 then completes in 262 s
   (incomplete after 1342 s before). The pattern (plain interval evaluation of a cancelling sum is
   useless; mean-value or point-plus-tiny-box forms are needed) is now the rule for this code.

Also: x = 100 (N = 1000, 4000 bits) was OOM-killed at the 10.7 GB cgroup limit; the Krawczyk step was
reworked to O(N^2) per iteration with a single precomputed `C0 = I - R J(y~)` and no per-iteration
matrices, and the certificate residual is formed by `arb_dot` without a product matrix; x = 80 then
ran at 2.4 GB where the old code was at 5.1 GB. The reference zeros beyond the 20th are now taken at
1200 bits up to the 400th (bounds down to 1e-350 stay measurable), 400 bits beyond.

Lessons recorded for the next person: `arb_printn` with few digits shows display rounding, not the
ball radius (three false alarms today); print `mag_printd(arb_radref(x))`. `pkill -f` on a pattern
that matches one's own shell kills the shell. Fully buffered output from a killed process leaves an
empty file: use `stdbuf -oL` for long runs.

Not done: mutation testing of the day's changes (the mutation script's `make clean` would remove the
binary the running grid uses); to run after the grid.

- **What generalises: the linear algebra, verbatim, and it is classical.** Group `Z`, window
  `{-M..M}`, Weil form = the notebook's own Toeplitz `Wform` (`thm:weil-positivity-finite`), the
  rank-two commutator = the rank-two displacement `Z T Z^* - T` of the cyclic shift, the Loewner
  matrix = a Loewner matrix on the circle, Lemma `key` = a new unitary key lemma
  (`U = Z^* - |Z^* xi><eta|`, `U^* T U = T` exactly, three lines), the circle conclusion =
  Caratheodory-Fejer 1911 = Connes-van Suijlekom's Corollary `corcar` (`CS:792`) = Makhoul 1981;
  `eps_M` = Pisarenko's noise floor; a Cayley transform makes the graph case an instance of
  `CS:1356`. 31 of 36 theory claims proved, all checked numerically, independently confirmed by
  the numerics lane (446 checks) and by my own numpy check.
- **What does not: the analytic content.** Finite divisor, so a critical window `K = R+1` where
  the construction is exact, under-resolved below, degenerate kernel above (even-simplicity
  fails); no second truncation, no Dirichlet-kernel approximation, no transcendental archimedean
  term (the graph's gamma factor is the rational `(1-u^2)^{|E|-|V|}` term), no regularised
  determinant, and no Hermite/prolate structure at all: the one step whose absence blocks CCM's
  proof is the one a finite graph cannot exercise.
- **What the graph adds.** The chain can be run on a false RH. For a non-Ramanujan graph the
  construction still returns a circle spectrum; `eps_M` crosses zero and diverges like
  `-m rho^{2M}` (slope recovers the offending eigenvalue); the even block stays PSD and the odd
  block goes negative, so `xi_min` turns odd and CCM's prescription fails while the kernel vector
  still recovers the divisor exactly. Reality is free for any Toeplitz matrix; positivity is the
  whole content; RH enters only as `eps -> 0`, Weil's criterion verbatim (CCM Cor. `strange`).
- **Sign and grading.** Ungraded graph: poles, sign flipped relative to CCM. Curve / purely odd
  divisor: CCM's sign, same code, and already in the literature (Hallouin-Perret 2019, cited at
  `CS:800`). Mixed divisor: indefinite by construction, the chain requires one parity.
- **Traps found.** `eta` is the delta at the window edge, not all-ones in the position basis (both
  lanes hit it; wrong `eta` gives real but wrong roots); the trivial `+-1` copies are not
  reciprocal closed and must be subtracted in the even extension; FLINT's
  `arb_fmpz_poly_complex_roots` hangs on repeated roots (square-free factor first);
  `acb_mat_eig_multiple_rump` fails at `n = 24` without precision doubling; double cannot certify
  `eps = 0` at the critical window (condition `1e16`-`1e18`), the argument for arb plus an exact
  rank stage.
- **Plan.** `ihz/`, sibling of `zst/`, `eigmin.c` ported verbatim, exact integer stage (Hashimoto,
  traces, Ihara-Bass as an exact polynomial identity, square-free charpoly for the truth
  spectrum), ball stage (Toeplitz form, certified eigenpair, roots on the circle by the
  theta-substitution, `U''` and the Cayley form as certified cross-checks, Prony multiplicities),
  milestones G1-G5, about 1400 lines and two weeks. Experiments: the under-resolved law (the one
  place the graph can inform zeta), the non-Ramanujan slope, irregular graphs (Weil positivity
  without duality), the curve sign, the mixed-divisor refusal, the anti-palindromic search.

is `notes/ccm-tensor-network.md`. Core conclusions:

- A retained metric-unitary transfer gives the Weil Gram of its open powers.
  Its conjugated-feature history has that matrix as a clock marginal; an
  explicit unary-clock MPS uses bond at most twice the distinct support size.
- The exact critical kernel gives the square-free support polynomial, which
  annihilates the original transfer only under semisimplicity. Multiplicities
  are separate weights. Supertraces require nonnegative net weights after the
  chosen sector rearrangement; unitary parity blocks alone do not suffice.
- The nullvector is an exact zero-energy clock vector only for the appropriate
  Gram. An unshifted radical gives a parent constraint for the original history;
  shifting the minimum changes the Gram and generally changes that history.
  A separate propagation Hamiltonian has the whole history as its ground state,
  with a unique line after pinning. No physical-site locality is inferred.
- CCM's rank-one boundary correction is self-adjoint in the shifted quotient
  metric. The killed vector is not a zero-frequency mode of that quotient.
  Exact finite intertwining with an existing transfer model is proved under
  zero unshifted minimum and the simple-radical hypotheses. Infinite HS Grams
  require smearing; the raw unitary trace diverges.

Adversarial repairs include the degenerate shifted-minimum counterexample,
the physical block-map adjoint, clock conjugation, nonempty feature space,
the precise Pauli edge-lift interpretation, Fourier-sign lockstep, and the
simple-radical condition for exact intertwining. Four stronger formulations
are retained as refuted claim rows. The physical Riemann bond and a controlled
