Findings while reading:

- The paper's displayed correction constant `c(L)` (integral of `(1 - e^{-x/2})/(e^x - e^{-x})`,
  `0.352` at `lambda = 3`) is not the one its own identity requires (`int (1 - e^{-x/2}) rho = 0.575`,
  closed form derived and checked by quadrature), and the definition of `gamma_L(n)` double counts it.
  The difference is `n`-independent: a multiple of the identity, so `xi` and the spectrum are
  unchanged and only `eps_N` shifts. Their table is reproduced exactly by the corrected version, so
  their code was right and the display is a typo.
- The secular function `sum_j xi_j/(j - s)` is not monotone between poles (`xi_j` changes sign);
  zeros 13 and 14 at `lambda = sqrt 13` share one pole interval. Root isolation must use the `2N` count
  as its completeness check; a naive grid missed 2-4 of 120 roots.
- Empirical: `|z_1 - gamma_1| ~ 5e4 (1 - chi_4(lambda))` and `eps_N ~ 10 (1 - chi_4(lambda))` with the
  Fuchs asymptotic the paper quotes, at both `lambda = 3` and `sqrt 13`: about `5.5` digits per unit of
  `x = lambda^2`, so `x = 100` should give hundreds of digits. That regime is what the C code is for.
- Structural reading for the programme: the construction is a machine from window explicit-formula
  data (atoms, kernels, trivial divisor) to a self-adjoint operator with provably real spectrum; on
  finite transfer operators it degenerates to Caratheodory-Fejer on Toeplitz matrices (exact once the
  window exceeds the divisor size), and on the modular surface it would take prime geodesics plus the
  cusp comb (shard 09c) and should return Maass `r_j` together with `gamma_n/2`, a computational form
  of H-CUSP-BRIDGE (shard 03f). Recorded in `plan.md` Sections 5-6.

## Afternoon: the vertical tracer-bullet MVP in C (`zst/`)

TJO: keep the work in this repo for now; "I like the plan in substance. Please impl a vertical tracer

Delivered `zst/` (C11, FLINT 3.0.1): `zst_riemann_ab` (Loewner data of the truncated Weil form, balls
with bounded series tails), even/odd blocks, `zst_eigmin` (inverse iteration on midpoints, then a
Krawczyk/Rump verification of the bordered eigen-system, contracted to the residual-limited width),
`zst_inertia_neg` (ball `LDL^T` inertia: certifies the paper's even-simple hypothesis as "exactly one
even eigenvalue below `2 eps_N`, no odd one"), `zst_secular_roots` (candidates = square roots of the
eigenvalues of the odd block of `D'^2`, `M_ij = i^2 delta_ij - 2 i xi_i j`, by approximate QR; each root
certified by interval Newton; completeness by the count `N` of pairwise disjoint roots), comparison
with `acb_dirichlet_zeta_zeros`; CLI `build/zst`; four test programs; two fuzz harnesses (libFuzzer +
ASan, and a seeded plain driver); `tools/mutate.py`; every formula cites
`refs/src/2511.22755/mc2arXiv.tex` by line or the mpmath prototype's run record.

**Result.** `./build/zst --x 13 --N 120 --prec 700 --zeros 50`: 5.0 s (mpmath: 108 s, uncertified).
`eps_N = 3.4839882e-59` certified to 1e-69; even-simple CERTIFIED; 120/120 roots; the paper's column
returned as certified upper bounds, `|z_1 - gamma_1| <= 2.44e-55`, `|z_10 - gamma_10| <= 3.98e-37`,
`|z_50 - gamma_50| <= 2.04e-3` (`notes/zeta-spectral-triples/zst_run_x13_N120.txt`). `x = 9, N = 40`:
0.19 s, first zero to `2.89e-34`.

**Three bugs, all found by red tests, and their lessons.** (1) arb's `acb_mat_eig_enclosure_rump`
fails unless the inverse iteration has converged below the even-block gap (`~4e-31`): four iterations
were not enough; now iterated to stationarity with one reused LU, and the enclosure is re-verified and
contracted by my own Krawczyk operator (the library returns its inflated box). (2) The normalisation
`sum_j xi_j = 1` amplifies the eigenvector by 1e18 (`x = 9`) to 1e28 (`x = 13`), the digits by which the
eigenfunction is small at the window edge; the secular function is then a difference of huge terms and
`arb_calc_isolate_roots` cannot certify signs on any sub-interval (a million unresolved blocks; a
mean-value form does not help since covering a unit interval would take ~1e9 blocks). Replaced by
point candidates plus tiny-interval Newton verification and completeness by count. (3) Two of the
`N = 40` positive roots lie at `s ~ 107` and `339`, far beyond the last pole `N`: a search range is the
wrong notion of completeness. Also: several apparent radius problems were `arb_printn` display rounding,
not ball widths; print `mag_printd(arb_radref(x))` when it matters.

**Fuzzing** found one real defect: `b_0` was a rounding neighbourhood of zero rather than exactly zero
(`b_n` is odd in `n`); fixed by symmetry. `fuzz_secular`: 66k inputs clean; `fuzz_ab`: 15k clean.

**Mutation testing** (`make mutate`, tests built under ASan): 27/30, then 35/40 (seed 1) and 29/40
(seed 2) killed. Two real gaps found and closed by red tests: the `j = N` branch beyond the last pole
(only exercised by roots too far out to feel the mutation; killed by a closed-form root at `s = 2` past
the pole at `N = 1`) and the contraction step of the interval Newton (candidates arrive polished, so it
was never stressed; killed by exposing `zst_secular_verify` and starting it 1e-6 off the root). The
remaining survivors were inspected one by one and are equivalent mutants, one candidate-matrix mutant
absorbed by the Newton polishing, and two out-of-bounds reads inside uninstrumented FLINT calls that
ASan cannot see (recorded as debt in `zst/README.md`).
