Findings while reading:

- The paper's displayed correction constant `c(L)` (integral of `(1 - e^{-x/2})/(e^x - e^{-x})`,
  `0.352` at `lambda = 3`) is not the one its own identity requires (`int (1 - e^{-x/2}) rho = 0.575`,
  closed form derived and checked by quadrature), and the definition of `gamma_L(n)` double counts it.
  The difference is `n`-independent: a multiple of the identity, so `xi` and the spectrum are
  unchanged and only `eps_N` shifts. Their table is reproduced exactly by the corrected version, so
  their code was right and the display is a typo.
- The secular function `sum_j xi_j/(j - s)` is not monotone between poles (`xi_j` changes sign);
  zeros 13 and 14 at `lambda = sqrt 13` share one pole interval. Root isolation must use the `2N` count
  as its completeness check; a naive grid missed 2-4 of 120 roots.
- Empirical: `|z_1 - gamma_1| ~ 5e4 (1 - chi_4(lambda))` and `eps_N ~ 10 (1 - chi_4(lambda))` with the
  Fuchs asymptotic the paper quotes, at both `lambda = 3` and `sqrt 13`: about `5.5` digits per unit of
  `x = lambda^2`, so `x = 100` should give hundreds of digits. That regime is what the C code is for.
- Structural reading for the programme: the construction is a machine from window explicit-formula
  data (atoms, kernels, trivial divisor) to a self-adjoint operator with provably real spectrum; on
  finite transfer operators it degenerates to Caratheodory-Fejer on Toeplitz matrices (exact once the
  window exceeds the divisor size), and on the modular surface it would take prime geodesics plus the
  cusp comb (shard 09c) and should return Maass `r_j` together with `gamma_n/2`, a computational form
  of H-CUSP-BRIDGE (shard 03f). Recorded in `plan.md` Sections 5-6.
