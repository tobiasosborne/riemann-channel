- Review finding: the page said "the 2n-dimensional space spanned by σf and τg". False: σ1 = τ1, so the labelling
  (f, g) ↦ σf + τg has rank 2n − 1 (2n − 2 when bipartite, σχ = −τχ). Checked numerically on K4, Petersen, cube, K5, prism21
  (ranks 7, 19, 14, 9, 83). The kernel is M-invariant: M(1, −1) = (1, −1), M(χ, χ) = −(χ, χ), the lines μ = ±1 of the trivial
  mode(s); B carries those eigenvalues on the cycle space instead (eigenvalue 1 has multiplicity |E| − |V| + 1 = g in all).
  Consequence worth stating: the phase space is the 2n-dimensional label space, not the subspace of the edge space, and Ω does
  not descend to the edge space (Ω non-degenerate, kernel non-zero). On the edge space B is self-adjoint for the symmetric
  pairing u·Fv (B^T = FBF); there is no symplectic form there. This is the "nullspace" TJO was sensing.
