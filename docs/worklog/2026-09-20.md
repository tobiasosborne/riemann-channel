Lindblad process whose absorption spectrum (Connes) is the zeros; the ring norm is a supertrace
because the auxiliary system is Bose--Fermi; RH is the Ramanujan property of that process, i.e. the

finite area through which only the angle-independent mode escapes, hence a one-dimensional exit;

## What was found

- **Prior art, decisive.** Arends--Peterson--Weich, arXiv:2603.26443 (2026), define resonances on
  geometrically finite graphs of groups by a resonance matrix that is exactly the rank-one perturbed
  Bass determinant `p` of the brief, compute two elliptic-curve tree quotients whose resonances are
  the square roots of the Hasse--Weil zeros on the circle `q^{-1/4}`, and leave the wave-equation /
  Lax--Phillips construction to follow-up work. Childs--Strouse, arXiv:1103.5077 (Levinson's theorem
  for graphs), contain the reflection coefficient of the brief verbatim, sign included, the
  bound / half-bound / confined classification, and the count via the winding of the phase. Kaneko--
  Koyama, arXiv:2303.09327, give the level-`A` constant term over `F_q[T]` as the zeta ratio; no
  source states that the `Gamma_0(N)` scattering matrix has Dirichlet `L`-zeros as poles (H-ARITH).
- **Corrections of the orchestrator's brief** (prover and numerics independently): the determinant
  form of `R` needs a minus sign (`R = -p/ptilde`, no power of `z`); the junction measure is
  `m_0 = 1`, `m_n = (q+1) q^{-n}`, and `c^2 = q+1` is right; cusp forms outside the band are `l^2` but
  invisible; threshold roots at `z = +-1` also cancel (Childs's half-bound states, on the critical
  line under `z = q^{s-1/2}`); the outgoing profile must start at `k >= 2`; the energy form is
  hyperbolic on bound-state data, and deleting them breaks the orthogonality of the projected
  radiation spaces (exact cross energy 3/32 on the pure `q = 2` cusp); the repair
  `D_+ = O_+ cap O_-^perp` gives `Theta = eta R B_bd`, the pole-removed inner part, `dim K = #res`,
  `spec Z` = resonances (not reflections); neither `R` nor `1/R` is the characteristic function with
  bound states present; `phi = 1/R`; uniqueness of the stationary density is unconditional and
  attraction needs aperiodicity, which fails iff `c = 1` when the rebound contains an eigenvector but
  can fail with nonreal resonances otherwise (`+-ir` example); the Gram identity
  `<k_n,k_m>(1 - conj z_n z_m) = conj(a_n) a_m` forces every mode to see the exit.
- **Structure.** RAM (Ramanujan diagram) and RH (all resonances on one circle) are independent
  already for one- and two-vertex cores; the product of all roots of `p` is exactly `1 - c^2`, so the
  common radius is fixed by the coupling and the visible bound states, never universally `q^{-1/4}`,
  and a full-weight cusp (`c^2 = q+1`) on a core whose only bound states are the Perron pair has no
  resonances at all (the pure cusp's emptiness is forced); the cusp sees only the Krylov space of the
  attachment vertex; `K_4` with `q = 2` satisfies RH (moduli 0.752259), the 3-cube and Petersen do not.
- **Transcription of 04h** to discrete time: CPTP renewal channel with explicit Kraus operators,
  finite mean always, mode-diagonal stationarity iff equal moduli with mean holding time
  `1/(1 - r^2)`, admissibility iff positive loss, flags realise every spectrum for every stable
  contraction, odd modes protected. A graph bound state is not a stationary even vacuum (time
  multipliers `beta^{+-1}`, indefinite block): the even exit is still missing.
- **Exposition.** `notes/cusp-graph/physics-picture.md` (Opus): the BSc scattering picture,
  Feshbach self-energy `c^2 z` vs `c^2/z`, quantum jumps = no-event contraction plus rebound, RH as
  equal linewidths with the functional equation as a width sum rule (paired widths add to 1/2, RH says
  no pair is split; equal width is not equal Q), the zeros as coherences; the primes live in the Wigner
  time delay. Upgraded to an interactive artifact (`physics-picture.html`) at TJO's request.
