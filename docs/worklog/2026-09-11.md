## Trajectory of the discussion

1. **Bost–Connes basics.** Primes as modes, ℓ²(ℕ) as bosonic Fock space,
   ζ(β) as the free-boson partition function, Euler product = product over
   modes. Correction: single-particle space is ℓ²(primes), not ℓ²(ℕ).
2. **Why not Wick-rotate to Hilbert–Pólya.** BC's spectrum {log n} and the
   zeros are Fourier dual (explicit formula), energies on one side are times
   on the other. TJO found this too jargon-laden; rebuilt from scratch.
3. **Elliptic curve over F₂** as a finite instance: gas over closed points
   (side A) vs Frobenius on H¹ (side B), linked by Lefschetz. Still too far.
4. **Permutations.** The one identity 1/det(1 − TM) = exp Σ Tr(Mⁿ)Tⁿ/n; a
   permutation's char poly is the Bose partition function with one mode per
   cycle. Finite permutations: RH is "unitary", automatic. Infinite
   permutations: the hard part is a *different* small matrix with the same
   fixed-point counts; exists iff Z is rational; RH is about the radii of its
   eigenvalues. Three shift examples (2 symbols; golden-mean; 4-symbol
   cyclic rule with eigenvalues 2, 1±i, 0) showing RH can hold or fail.
5. **Automata / Bowen–Lanford**, rationality results (Manning, Dwork,
   Puri–Ward realisability).
6. **Actual RH as a flow** with orbit periods log p; inferences: entropy 1,
   Lyapunov 1, T log T Weyl law, absorption sign, archimedean term; Selberg
   as the one family where all of this is realised and RH is a theorem.
7. **Ihara zeta of regular graphs** as the vestigial Selberg: Ihara–Bass
   compression, RH ⇔ Ramanujan; K₄ satisfies, prism C₁₆×K₂ fails
   (computed). Cohomology of the graph made explicit (Bass blocks); the
   existence of side B is cheap, the radius bound is the content.
8. **Shor**: U_a is BC's level-N Galois symmetry and a snapshot of the
   dilation flow; periods tied to Dirichlet L zeros.
9. **Quantum expanders → quantum Ihara.** Edge superoperator on M_n ⊗ C^D,
   Ihara–Bass verified numerically, RH ⇔ Hastings-Ramanujan; identified as
   the Artin–Ihara L-function of the bouquet graph twisted by Ad(U).
10. **MPS.** Channel = MPS transfer matrix; Tr Eⁿ = PBC ring norm; zeta =
    generating function of ring norms; AKLT = K₄; side B = the transfer
    matrix. Fock structure: direct sum over sizes is the single-ring
    space, the gas is the symmetric Fock space over it; same ζ as a Bose gas
    of $-\log E$ (Feynman cycles). Note: the conversation called $-\log E$ the
    "entanglement Hamiltonian"; that is a misnomer corrected in
    `notes/what-rh-has-become.md` §8 — the transfer generator is non-Hermitian
    and the zeros' imaginary parts are its oscillation frequencies.
11. **cMPS / Selberg** (flagged by TJO as overreach on the QFT side).
12. **BC as MPO over the prime chain** (computed): bond ℤ/b, Shor map as
    transfer, L(β,χ̄)/ζ(β) eigenvalues, KMS boundary vectors, β=1 collapse.
13. **The sought object**: a cMPS whose rings are the primes and whose
    transfer generator has the zeros; PNT = primitivity, RH = one
    correlation length. Space forced to a ℚ^×-quotient of adelic geometry
    (product formula = flatness); expander = compression = Lax–Phillips.
14. **Holevo/Werner lift**: Lévy–Khinchin form for dilation-covariant
    semigroups; Khinchin infinite divisibility of the zeta distribution;
    dilation theorems give existence, not positivity.
15. **"Go hard" computation**: scattering symbol S(τ), its zeros, innerness,
    the σ=1 prime-phase identity, and the 3000-zero ring-norm test. Note
    written and rendered.

## Afternoon: Weil–LPS channels computed

TJO asked for the proposed object to be computed outright. Done for seven
primes p and the admissible q (q ≡ 1 mod 4, q a square mod p; the Weil
intertwiner needs determinant one). Bugs found and fixed on the way: the
quaternion enumeration started at an odd coordinate for some q (caught by
the σ(n) count); the full-space channel had parity as a second fixed point
(resolved by splitting into the two irreducible Weil blocks); the Hecke
relation failed on SL₂ by signs (resolved by working on PSL₂). All checks
green afterwards; see `notes/weil-lps-channels.md` and `outputs/weil_lps_*`.
An algebraic-integer recognition of the joint eigenvalues from 5-digit
output was attempted and discarded as inconclusive and, on reflection,
tautological (integer adjacency matrix).

## Evening: MPS formulation of the Weil conjectures

TJO asked whether the Weil conjectures admit an MPS formulation this way.
Answer worked out and computed: for quadratic Artin–Schreier curves, yes,
exactly, with RH manifest as unitarity of the transfer matrix; beyond
quadratic the shift basis loses locality and the uniform object is Dwork's
p-adic transfer operator, which cannot see RH. Bugs on the way: a wrong
irreducibility test for composite n (replaced by sympy's gf_irreducible_p);
the even-n sign, which turned out to be the L-function normalisation, not
a bug. See `notes/artin-schreier-mps.md`.
