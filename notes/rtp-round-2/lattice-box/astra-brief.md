## 0. Setting

Window `[0, L]`, `L = log x`, Fourier modes `|n| <= N`. For a real distribution `D` on `[0, L]` the Loewner data
are `b_n = -(1/pi) D(sin omega_n y)`, `a_n = 2 D((1 - y/L) cos omega_n y)`, `omega_n = 2 pi n/L`, and the form is
`H = H0 + T(w)`, where `H0` is the Loewner form of the pole plus archimedean data (zeta) and `T(w) = sum_k w_k T_k`,
`T_k` the Loewner form of the point mass `-delta(y - y_k)` at a position `y_k = log n_k` in `(0, L]` (sign as in
`zst_weil_t`: atom weights `-Lambda(n) n^{-1/2}`; fix the sign convention from `zst.h` and lane B2, and keep it).
Truth: `w*_k = Lambda(n_k)/sqrt(n_k)` (zero at composite non-prime-powers). The lattice set is
`P_N^lat = {w in R^m : H0 + T(w) >= 0}`, a closed convex spectrahedron containing `w*` (lane A1 certifies
`H(w*) > 0` at `x = 13`, `N <= 200`, with `eps_N` down to `3.5e-59` at `N = 120`).

Round 1 found (`x = 13`, `m = 11`, positions `log 2 .. log 12`) that `P_N^lat` is a tiny box around `w*` once

mechanism offered: near-singularity of the true form (16 eigenvalues below `1e-6` against 11 unknowns). Nothing
was proved.

## 1. Statements to prove or correct (theory)

for `v` in the window space is `-sum_k delta_k q_v(y_k)` where `q_v(y) = (f_v^* * f_v)(y) + (f_v^* * f_v)(-y)` is the
symmetrised autocorrelation of the trigonometric polynomial `f_v = sum v_n V_n` (check this against the formula
sheet; `q` evaluated at the atoms is exactly how the atoms enter `D(q)`). Then the question is whether the cone

window edge, where `(1 - y/L)` kills the `a_n` contribution) and rational relations `y_j = 2 y_k` (prime powers).

L2 (dual certificates and an a priori width bound). Let `(lambda_i, v_i)` be an eigen-decomposition of

state and prove it); (ii) the exact box `[w_k^-, w_k^+]` of `P_N^lat` in coordinate `k` is the value of the SDP

conditioning of the `r x m` sensitivity matrix `g` restricted to those directions. Give the sharpest bound you can
prove, and say exactly which spectral quantity controls the width: `eps_N` alone, the `m` smallest eigenvalues, or a
condition number of `g`. This decides how the width should scale with `x`.

whose window form is positive semidefinite on all trigonometric polynomials on `[0, L]`. Prove or refute:
