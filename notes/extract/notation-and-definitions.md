## Ambiguities the orchestrator must decide

1. **Channel normalisation: `$\Phi$` vs `$D\Phi$` vs `$\Sigma$`.** QIG §1 lists this explicitly as an unfixed choice ("normalisation of the channel (`$\sum_i\mathcal E_i$` versus `$\frac1D\sum_i\mathcal E_i$`); the notebook elsewhere uses `$D\Phi=\sum_i\mathcal E_i$`"). PAQ §0 and HO item 1 define `$\Phi$` *with* the `$1/D$`, WRH §4 *without*, WLP §1 with `$1/(q+1)$`. Every Ihara–Bass statement in the corpus is written with `$D\Phi$`. Decide one and rewrite the others.
2. **Ramanujan units.** `$\lambda_2$` of a channel vs `$(q+1)\lambda_2$` in adjacency units vs Hastings' `$\lambda_H=2\sqrt{D-1}/D$` vs the bare `$2\sqrt q$`. WLP §2's table mixes both units in one row. Pick one reporting convention for the lab book and convert the tables.
3. **`$q$` vs `$D-1$`.** The graph degree parameter, the field size, the LPS parameter and `$D-1$` are all written `$q$` in different notes. They coincide in the LPS story and do *not* coincide in general (a `$D$`-Kraus channel has `$q=D-1$` but no field). Decide whether the lab book uses `$q$` uniformly with a standing identification, or reserves `$q$` for field/graph and always writes `$D-1$` for channels.
4. **`$\xi$`: completed zeta vs correlation length.** Both are load-bearing and both appear in WRH §4–§6 within two pages. One must be renamed.
5. **`$\psi$`: five meanings** (Chebyshev, additive character, Lévy exponent, digamma, MPS ring state). At least three renames are needed; propose `\cheb`, `\addchar`, `\levy`, `\digam`, `\ringstate`.
6. **`$T$`: three meanings** (non-backtracking operator, Hilbert–Pólya/transfer generator, curve-zeta variable + Weyl operator + Hecke operator). The non-backtracking operator is the most frequent; the generator of WRH §5, §7 should probably be renamed.
7. **`$\rho$`: three meanings** (zeta zero, density matrix, twist/holonomy). DVG and the channel notes will sit in adjacent chapters.
8. **`$\beta$`: three meanings** (real part of a zero, BC inverse temperature, Deligne weight). RCN's title is "Bost–Connes at `$\beta=1$`" and WRH §5 writes `$\rho=\beta+i\gamma$`; both are in the same story.
