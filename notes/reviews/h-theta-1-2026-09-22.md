## Assessment

The refutation of `conj:h-theta-1` is sound and complete. It rests on two exact facts, both verified here
independently of the author's scripts: the Gram matrix of Burnol's level-one evaluators at critical-line
points is real (an antiunitary symmetry `A = F_+ conj` fixing those evaluators, with no analytic input beyond
the continuity of the evaluations and the functional equation `M(F_+ f) = M(f)(1 - .)`), and a positive
rank-one matrix has a positive cyclic product while the loss factors `2a + i(b_j - b_i)` have a cyclic
product with imaginary part `-q_12 q_23 q_31 != 0`. Three distinct critical-line zeros exist
unconditionally, so the scalar-exit requirement of the conjecture fails in Burnol's norm without RH,
without simplicity and without any numerical value. The numerical Gram (`O`, loss spectrum with a negative
eigenvalue `-0.0924`) is reproduced to every printed digit by an independent implementation and supports the
stronger, correctly-labelled-OPEN statement that not even a contraction realisation exists. The author's 28
corrections to the brief are all justified; the ones that matter mathematically (the half-plane shift
between the de Branges denominator and the decay denominator, `L_1` versus `K_1`, the two zero
dictionaries, the origin of the exponent 2, the full rank of finite-time defects) are each confirmed above.
