**"reversal of words is a bijection of cyclically non-backtracking words because `σ` is an
involution".** Correct. With `w = (i_1,…,i_ℓ)` admissible iff `i_{k+1} ≠ σ(i_k)` for `k ∈ ℤ/ℓ`,

commutes with rotation classes. Checked exhaustively at `D = 6` for `ℓ = 2,3,4,5`
(|S| = 30, 126, 630, 3126): the set is reversal-stable in every case ✓.
