A quasi-Riemann hypothesis for Dedekind zeta functions

Abstract

Recent work on Artin's primitive root conjecture establishes a zero-free half-plane Re s>1−10−6\mathrm{Re}\,s>1-10^{-6}Res>1−10−6 for finite-order Hecke LLL-functions over cyclotomic fields containing e2πi/12e^{2\pi i/12}e2πi/12. Following their strategy, we prove that if KKK is any number field, then the Dedekind zeta function ζK(s)\zeta_K(s)ζK​(s) does not vanish in the half-plane Re s>31/32\mathrm{Re}\,s>31/32Res>31/32. As a corollary, the LLL-function of any finite-order Hecke character defined over KKK also does not vanish in the half-plane Re s>31/32\mathrm{Re}\,s > 31/32Res>31/32, wherever it is holomorphic. These results were proved and formalized in Lean using AxiomProver. Compared to the work on Artin's primitive root conjecture, the hardest part was the formalization of the work of Kazhdan and Patterson on metaplectic forms in order to accommodate all number fields.
