was previously used by Heath-Brown in his work on primes in short intervals [HB88]. Our
work is the first to execute this idea in the context of an oscillating sequence with a main
term of density X ´δ .
   Finally we note that our Type-II bound (as stated) is not able to handle the narrow
range X 1{2´ε ď A, B ď X 1{2`ε . So instead in this range we use the same kind of ideas
that we used to handle Type-III sums. This is not necessary and we could have obtained
10                      ALEXANDER DUNN AND MAKSYM RADZIWILL

a power-saving in this range with a little bit more work. However, this wouldn’t have
made a significant difference so we refrained from this additional work.

Remark 5.5. The functional equation in [Pat77, Theorem 6.1] could be potentially used
to obtain a functional equation for (5.64) similar to the one presented below in Proposition

of twisted cubic Gauss sums that involve the ramified prime, and this obscures the root
number. We found it advantageous to work directly with the automorphy of the cubic
theta function (this is a more advanced starting point since the purpose of [Pat77] was to
compute the Fourier coefficients of the cubic theta function).
