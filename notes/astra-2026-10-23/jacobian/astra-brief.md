Π_λ (x² − λx + p) over the eigenvalues λ of A_s. s is Ramanujan if every λ (there is no trivial mode if the flux is non-trivial on
some cycle; say precisely when) satisfies |λ| < 2√p strictly. Then no factor has a real root, so by Centeleghe–Stix (T, F) =
(Z^{2n}, M_s) is an abelian variety A_{Y,s} over F_p of dimension n, canonically up to the equivalence of categories, and its
L-polynomial is P_{Y,s}(u) = det(1 − u M_s) = Π_λ (1 − λu + pu²) = the super-Ihara numerator (the Ihara zeta's reciprocal without
the tree factor). Note the sign flip: for a curve, N_k = p^k + 1 − Σ α_i^k with α_i the roots of the reciprocal L-polynomial;
here the roots μ of x² − λx + p play the role of the α_i.
