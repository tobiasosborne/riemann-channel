### G1-T1-2 RH for a finite permutation is free; the content is the *other* small matrix
- Absorbs: L01-009, L01-010, L01-011, L02-003, L12-003
- Class: EXPLORED-registered
- Raised by: TJO (as a question), answered by the orchestrator
- What: A permutation matrix permutes an orthonormal basis, so all its eigenvalues are on the unit circle and the "RH analogue" holds for free. The content appears only for infinite permutations, where one asks for a different, *small* matrix `M` with `Tr M^l = fix(sigma^l)` for every `l`; such a matrix exists exactly when the zeta is rational. Its eigenvalues are new numbers and need not be unimodular: for `x -> x^2` on the closure of `F_2`, `fix(sigma^n) = 2^n` and the small matrix is `[2]`. So "RH = unit circle" does not survive, and RH becomes a statement about the *radii* of the small matrix's eigenvalues relative to the leading one. One infinite cycle (the shift on `Z`) is empty (`Z(T)=1`, no fixed points); infinitely many finite cycles is the real case.
- Lead: "given the counts, construct the small matrix and bound its radii" is the whole notebook in miniature.
- Where: `transcript/transcript.md`:296-316; `report/sections/02_definitions.tex`:39-47; `docs/worklog/2026-09-11.md`:17-23

### G1-T1-6 Side B is a *dual*, not a rotation: Fourier duality and the absorption sign
- Absorbs: L01-003, L01-004, L01-005, L02-001, L12-048
- Class: EXPLORED-negative
- Raised by: TJO (the Wick-rotation proposal), corrected by the orchestrator
- What: TJO asked whether going to real time in the Bost-Connes trace gives Hilbert-Polya. It does not: `Tr(e^{-H/2} e^{-itH}) = zeta(1/2+it)` is a damped return amplitude, so the zeros are special *times* of the BC flow and never eigenvalues of `H`, whose spectrum is `{log n}`. - Lead: any model of side B must reproduce the absorption sign; this steering motivated the adele-class-space direction and every later grading argument.

### G1-T2-5 The master question: where does the unitarity come from, and must it?
- Absorbs: L01-099, L01-105, L01-119, L12-049
- Class: PARTIAL
- Raised by: orchestrator (README working picture), taken up as the repository's stated master question
- What: Sorting the existing proofs by where the unitarity comes from: Artin-Schreier (Parseval for additive characters, the Hilbert-Polya operator explicit); Weil for curves (Hodge index on `C x C`, Rosati positivity); Deligne (positivity of even tensor powers, fixed loss paid by a base curve, roots as `k -> infinity`, *no* Hermitian form); LPS graphs (inherited from Deligne); random graphs and interlacing families (trace method / real-rootedness, no Hilbert-Polya operator). ### G1-T3-1 The quantum Ihara zeta: RH iff Ramanujan quantum expander
- Absorbs: L01-021, L01-023, L02-008, L12-006
- Class: EXPLORED-registered
- Raised by: TJO (the quantum-expander proposal), proved by the orchestrator
- What: For a unital channel with `D` unitary Kraus operators closed under adjoint, define the quantum Hashimoto operator `T(rho (x) |i>) = sum_{j != ibar} U_i rho U_i^dag (x) |j>` on `M_n (x) C^D`.
