## prop:ccm-tn-pauli — retained F5 companion, metric, and kernel

ASSUME the two retained values are (-1+2i)/sqrt(5) and
(-1-2i)/sqrt(5), the net support of the project's graded nonbacktracking
Pauli/F5 edge lift after even/odd cancellations. These are not the raw
doubled adjacency eigenvalues, which are 6,-2,-2,-2.
Use the following companion representative, independently of an MPS gauge:

    U = [[0,-5],[1,-2]]/sqrt(5),   G = [[1,-1],[-1,5]].

PROVE that the three-length Weil matrix is the Gram matrix of I,U,U^2
in the G-induced operator inner product, its kernel encodes the minimal
polynomial of U, and the native Hilbert--Schmidt identification is false.

<1>1. G is positive definite: its leading minors are 1 and 4. Direct
multiplication gives U^* G U = G. Thus the G-adjoint U^sharp = G^{-1}U^*G
equals U^{-1}. This computation does not use a spectral conjecture.

<1>2. The power traces t_k = Tr U^k obey
t_0=2, t_1=-2/sqrt(5), t_2=-6/5 and
t_{k+2}=-(2/sqrt(5))t_{k+1}-t_k. Indeed direct multiplication gives
U^2+(2/sqrt(5))U+I=0. Since U is not scalar, this quadratic is minimal.

<1>3. With W_jk=t_{k-j}, j,k=0,1,2 and the Hermitian extension,

    W = [[2,-2/sqrt(5),-6/5],
         [-2/sqrt(5),2,-2/sqrt(5)],
         [-6/5,-2/sqrt(5),2]].

For these real traces the alternative transpose convention is identical.
Its characteristic polynomial has roots 0,14/5,16/5, as follows by
factoring det(sI-W). Its radical is the line generated by
v=(1,2/sqrt(5),1)^T, verified by Wv=0 and the two positive eigenvalues.

<1>4. In the inner product Tr(A^sharp B), Step <1>1 gives
Tr((U^j)^sharp U^k)=Tr U^{k-j}=W_jk. Therefore the operator relation
in Step <1>2 is exactly the vanishing feature superposition specified by
the kernel vector in Step <1>3.

<1>5. The ordinary HS norm squared of U is Tr(U^*U)=6, whereas W_11=2.
The native HS Gram of I,U,U^2 is therefore not W. The positive metric is
an essential datum, not an omitted harmless normalisation.

<1>6. The original unnormalised curve counts are
N_k=1+5^k-((-1+2i)^k+(-1-2i)^k). Moving the known even contribution
1+5^k across and dividing by 5^(k/2) gives t_k. In particular N_0=0,
N_1=8,N_2=32, and this identity specifies the sign before building W.
It does not assert that the full graded form is positive.

## Guide to what the example reconstructs

The companion representative has two distinct eigenvalues, so its cyclic
algebra has dimension two and multiplication by U reconstructs that support.
This fact does not recover the six original Pauli letters or the physical
qubit auxiliary space. They supply a realisation of the same retained data.
In a repeated-eigenvalue example the cyclic dimension counts distinct support,
while the Gram weights retain spectral multiplicity. With a Jordan block,
the spectral annihilator need not annihilate the original operator; the
existing claim `prop:weil-blind-jordan` already proves that obstruction.
